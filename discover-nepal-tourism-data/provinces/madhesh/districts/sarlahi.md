## Coverage notes

- Bharat Tal is listed separately from the wider lakeside recreation zone because one is the waterbody and the other is the visitor-use area.
- Murtiya’s archaeological ruins, Mukteshwar Temple and nearby ponds are separate records, but the ponds remain ground-verification items.
- Sagarnath Forest is retained as an established forest destination; smaller nearby forest routes are not treated as confirmed attractions without local checks.
- No exact coordinates are assigned until the actual entrance, shoreline, shrine or ruin has been mapped.

## Sources used for this expansion

- Department of Archaeology-derived monument references for the Murtiya temple ruins.
- Bagmati Municipality and widely reported references for Bharat Tal.
- District and local references for Sagarnath Forest, Nunthar, Nadhi Tal, Murtiya and major river systems.
