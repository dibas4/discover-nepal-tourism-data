## Coverage notes

- Salahesh Fulbari, its seasonal orchid phenomenon, Salahesh religious sites and the reported palace/gadhi landscape are separated because they represent different tourism products.
- Smaller lakes and ponds are deliberately retained with “needs local verification” status because water extent, maintenance and access can change rapidly.
- Patari Mela is treated as an event-linked cultural place, not a permanently operating attraction.
- The Kamala and Balan river corridors require seasonal safety and access information before public guide publication.

## Source basis for this expansion

- Government-recognized cultural-monument references for Siraha district.
- Established documentation of Salahesh Fulbari and the annual cultural fair.
- District and municipality references identifying Kamal Daha, Haathi Daha, Dhamini Than, Patari Mela, Aakash Ganga and the Chure belt as tourism-potential locations.
- Government customs and district references for Thadi as a historic border gateway.
