## Coverage notes

- Tilaurakot is treated as a heritage landscape with separate records for the city, gates, ramparts and museum because they are distinct visitor and research assets.
- Kudan, Gotihawa, Niglihawa, Araurakot, Sagarhawa and Sisaniya are separate archaeological destinations, not interchangeable labels for the same circuit.
- Jagadishpur Reservoir is separated from its birdwatching product because the wetland and the wildlife-tourism experience require different data fields later.
- Small stupa mounds, ponds and village shrines mentioned in local lists are not added as fully established records until their coordinates and surviving condition are confirmed.
- Cross-border Piprahwa in India is not included as a Nepal destination; only Nepal-side archaeological names are retained where local identification is still being checked.

## Source and verification status

- Tilaurakot is on Nepal’s UNESCO World Heritage Tentative List and is protected under Nepal’s ancient-monument framework.
- Major Buddhist sites are supported by Lumbini Development Trust, Department of Archaeology and established Greater Lumbini heritage references.
- Jagadishpur Reservoir is an established wetland and bird habitat; water level, bird season, shoreline access and visitor facilities remain time-sensitive.
