## Coverage and boundary notes

- Ramgrama Stupa, its archaeological landscape, buried monastery evidence and active pilgrimage area are related but distinct records and should not be collapsed into one generic attraction.
- The stupa is on UNESCO’s Tentative List, not the inscribed World Heritage List.
- Triveni Dham is frequently described under the historic undivided Nawalparasi district. Following the 2015–2017 administrative split, the principal temple/confluence complex is associated with Binayi Triveni Rural Municipality in Nawalpur (East). Only the western Susta–Gandak approaches are retained here.
- Valmiki Ashram should not be assigned to Nawalparasi (West) without precise national-boundary and access verification.
- Daunne ridge destinations can be confused across older district descriptions; exact ward and municipal boundaries should be confirmed before coordinates are published.
- Small lakes, wetlands, parks and fort remains are inventory candidates, not ground-validated visitor products.

- UNESCO World Heritage Centre tentative-list documentation for Ramagrama, the relic stupa of Lord Buddha.
- Nepal Department of Archaeology research and published excavation references for Ramgrama.
- Greater Lumbini Area archaeological research and Lumbini Development Trust heritage context.
