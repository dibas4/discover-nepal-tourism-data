## Coverage notes

- The **Medieval Town of Tansen** and the **Rishikesh Complex of Ruru Kshetra** are separate UNESCO Tentative List properties; neither is currently an inscribed World Heritage Site.
- Tansen Durbar, Mul Dhoka, Sitalpati, Bhagwati Temple, Amar Narayan Temple, Srinagar and Purankot are retained as distinct visitor records within the wider Tansen heritage landscape.
- Rani Mahal, its museum use, and the surrounding Rani Ghat/Rani Ban landscape are separated because they have different operational and environmental verification needs.
- Ridi and Ramdi riverbank records need seasonal verification because floods, erosion, temporary structures and ritual access can change.
- Nuwakot and Bakumgadhi are not yet suitable for detailed public guide pages until exact coordinates, surviving remains, road access and local names are confirmed.

## Sources used for this first inventory

- UNESCO Tentative List documentation for the Medieval Town of Tansen and Rishikesh Complex of Ruru Kshetra.
- Tansen Municipality tourism listings and municipal heritage references.
- Nepal Tourism Board and established Nepal heritage references for Tansen, Srinagar, Rani Mahal, Bhairabsthan and Palpa cultural tourism.
- District and local-level references for Rambha Devi, Satyawati, Barangdi, Argali, Madanpokhara, Rampur and historic forts.
