## Coverage notes

- The UNESCO property is recorded at both complex level and component level because visitors and future CMS pages may search for each element separately.
- International monasteries are currently grouped by monastic zone; individual monastery records should be added later from the official Lumbini Development Trust inventory.
- Devdaha, Bhawanipur, Kanyamai and Sainamaina require archaeological-site boundaries, coordinates and conservation-status checks before detailed publication.
- Siddha Baba is a cross-boundary destination and must not be assigned to Rupandehi without confirming the exact feature or visitor entrance being described.
- Gajedi Lake, Tinau riverbanks and Chhapiya facilities require recurring local verification because boating, flood conditions and commercial operations can change.

## Sources used for this first expansion

- UNESCO World Heritage Centre record for Lumbini, including the Maya Devi Temple remains, Ashoka Pillar, Shakya Tank/Puskarini, ancient viharas and stupas.
- Government of Nepal, Lumbini Development Trust, for the Lumbini Master Plan, Sacred Garden, monastic zones, Cultural Centre and New Lumbini Village.
- Municipal and heritage references for Devdaha, Sainamaina, Jitgadhi, Manimukunda Sen Park, Gajedi Lake and Chhapiya.
