## Verification model

- **UNESCO-confirmed / government-managed:** recognized heritage or protected sites supported by authoritative records.
- **Established:** widely recognized destination, but coordinates, ward or operating details may still be pending.
- **First-round researched:** plausible and relevant inventory record requiring stronger source or municipality-level confirmation.
- **Needs local verification:** changing natural feature, small structure, business-led attraction or uncertain mapped location.
