# Quality Flags and Data Rules

This folder organizes the first-round research already completed in chat. It is not yet a publication-ready verified database.

## Verify before publishing

1. Exact municipality and ward.
2. Latitude and longitude.
3. Duplicate sites that cross district or municipal boundaries.
4. Alternate spellings and local-language names.
5. Whether a site is currently open and accessible.
6. Whether a project is complete, proposed or still developing.
7. Whether a place is a public attraction, gateway, commercial business, tourism corridor or district-level landscape.

## Shared master places

Protected areas and landscape systems that cross district boundaries should use one master Place ID:

- Koshi Tappu Wildlife Reserve
- Tinjure–Milke–Jaljale corridor
- Mundhum Trail
- Makalu Barun National Park
- Kanchenjunga Conservation Area
- Sagarmatha National Park

## Priority interpretation

- Major: deserves a primary destination or cluster page.
- High: strong supporting page or itinerary stop.
- Emerging: retain for later verification and possible local page.
- Developing: project or attraction not yet confirmed as fully operational.
- Gateway: access/service location, not necessarily a sightseeing attraction.
