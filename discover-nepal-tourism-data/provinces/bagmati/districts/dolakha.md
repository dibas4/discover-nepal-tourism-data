## Coverage notes

- The former combined entry **“Lamabagar and Lapchi valley”** has been separated because they are distinct destinations.
- The former combined entry **“Biguti and Tamakoshi valleys”** has been replaced with clearer Bigu and Tamakoshi records; “Biguti” appears to be an inconsistent spelling.
- Mountain peaks requiring technical climbing permits are not listed as ordinary sightseeing places unless they also function as established trekking destinations.
- Cross-district Sailung is retained with a border note rather than assigned exclusively to Dolakha.
- Small local temples, waterfalls and picnic sites should not be added until their exact name, municipality and tourism relevance can be verified.

## Sources used for this expansion

- District and municipality structure: Government local-level records and Dolakha district references.
- Kalinchowk Bhagwati Temple and cable-car access: published district/tourism references and national reporting.
- Dolakha Bhimsen Temple and historic Dolakha: heritage and temple references.
- Gaurishankar Conservation Area, Rolwaling Valley and Tsho Rolpa: conservation and trekking references.
- Jiri, Cherdung, Kalo Bhir and Ramite Danda: Jiri municipal/destination references.
- Kalinag and Chaughara: Sunkhani/Kalinchowk local references.
