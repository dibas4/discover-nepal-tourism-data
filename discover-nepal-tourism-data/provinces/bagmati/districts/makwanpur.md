## Coverage notes

- Indra Sarovar, Markhu and the dam viewpoint are separate visitor products within the same reservoir landscape.
- Chitlang Village, its hiking route and its farm experiences are kept as distinct tourism records.
- Parsa National Park is cross-district and should be linked to the park’s primary destination page rather than duplicated as a full standalone district product.
- Historic ropeway remains require exact structure-level mapping before publication.
