## Coverage notes

- Nuwakot Durbar complex, Saat Tale Durbar and Nuwakot old town are separate records but belong to one heritage cluster.
- Devighat and the Prithvi Narayan Shah memorial area are separate religious and historical visitor products.
- Kakani is included as a hill station, while its memorial park is retained as a separate site.
- Langtang and Shivapuri protected-area records are cross-district links and should point to shared destination pages.
