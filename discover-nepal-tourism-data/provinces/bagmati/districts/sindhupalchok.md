## Coverage notes

- The former combined entry **“Melamchi–Ghyangfedi villages”** has been replaced by distinct Helambu settlements where the place identity is clearer.
- Panch Pokhari itself and the Panch Pokhari trekking route are retained separately because one is a sacred lake destination and the other is a multi-stage route product.
- Bhote Koshi rafting, canyoning and bungee activities are separate tourism products rather than one generic river entry.
- Cross-district Langtang National Park is retained with a geographic-sector note and should not be presented as belonging exclusively to Sindhupalchok.
- Ne-Pemasal, Kshemadevi, Kafeshwar Mahadev and parts of Listikot should not receive full public guide pages until exact local mapping and names are verified.
