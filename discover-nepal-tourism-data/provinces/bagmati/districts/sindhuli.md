## Coverage notes

- Sindhuligadhi Fort, its viewpoint and the war memorial are separate visitor products within one heritage landscape.
- Junar orchards are treated as agro-tourism rather than a single conventional attraction.
- BP Highway roadside stops are included only where they have clear visitor use or scenic significance.
- Betini Tal and smaller local shrines remain verification records, not publication-ready destinations.
