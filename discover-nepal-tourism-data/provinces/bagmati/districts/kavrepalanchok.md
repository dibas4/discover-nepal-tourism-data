## Coverage notes

- Namo Buddha and Thrangu Tashi Yangtse Monastery are separate but connected visitor products.
- Panauti town, its museum, temple and sacred confluence are maintained as distinct records within one heritage cluster.
- Bethanchok Narayanthan is religious; Bethanchok Hill is the broader viewpoint and hiking destination.
- Nagarkot’s eastern ridge is cross-district and should link to a shared Nagarkot destination page.
- Smaller waterfalls, caves and local viewpoints are excluded until they can be mapped and verified reliably.
