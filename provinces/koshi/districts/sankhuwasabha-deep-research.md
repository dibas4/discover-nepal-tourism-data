## QA decisions
- Makalu Barun National Park is the parent protected landscape; Barun Valley, Makalu Base Camp, Shipton La, Seduwa and Tashigaon are distinct child route/settlement nodes.

- Arun Valley and Tinjure–Milke–Jaljale are landscape/corridor objects crossing multiple local jurisdictions.
- Kimathanka is a border settlement; border access is never assumed open to tourists.
- TIMS/guide rules are explicitly treated as current regulation data, not permanent prose.
- Fort and local-park records remain PARTIAL/CURRENT CHECK where exact heritage/public-access evidence is weaker.
