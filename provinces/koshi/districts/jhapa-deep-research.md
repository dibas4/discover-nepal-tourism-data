## QA decisions
- Domukha is an official riverfront tourism area; Kankai Dham/Kotihom is a distinct religious complex in the broader Kankai river corridor.
- Kechana Kawal is the geographic lowland/landmark area; Kechana Jhil is the wetland node. Do not duplicate them as identical pins.
- Bahundangi Elephant Corridor is an ecological/human-wildlife interface, not an entertainment wildlife attraction.
- Kakarbhitta is a border gateway; immigration/customs rules never belong as static copy.
- Private tea estates require explicit visitor permission/current operating verification.
- Developing Damak/Birat Buddha project stays HOLD until public opening is confirmed.
- Religious legends at Satakshi/Chillagadh/Dhanushkoti should be presented as beliefs/traditions, not archaeological fact without evidence.
