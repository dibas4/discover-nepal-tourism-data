- Olangchung Gola and Tiptala are trans-Himalayan/border nodes; crossing/access rules must be live-verified.
- Timbung Pokhari requires cross-district GIS normalization.
- Caves, hot springs and waterfalls carry access/safety fields rather than generic activity promises.
