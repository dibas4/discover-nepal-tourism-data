# Udayapur — Visitor Ready (14/14)

## Introduction & history
Udayapur is a transition district between the eastern Terai/Chure and middle hills, with historic forts, sacred ponds, river landscapes and a northern view toward the Himalaya. Gaighat/Triyuga is the practical service hub.

Udayapurgadhi and Chaudandigadhi = historic fort/viewpoint AREAS. Rauta Pokhari + Rauta Mahadev = lake/pilgrimage cluster with WATER/AREA and shrine POI. Tapli Pokhari = sacred water/viewpoint AREA. Limchungbung/Debate Danda = Himalayan viewpoint AREA. Triveni Dham = river-confluence pilgrimage AREA. Koshi Tappu Udayapur sector = access/relationship sector of the shared reserve, never a separate reserve. Sapta Koshi = river CORRIDOR with verified bank/access nodes. Basaha Than Shivalaya, Dwardani Devi, Kanya Aulshree Gumba, Lingeshwar Shivalaya and Than Pokhari Than = religious/cultural POIs/areas. Mini Apraha Waterfall = waterfall POI with entrance/trailhead GIS required.

No restricted-area trekking permit. Reserve rules apply only when entering the protected area; temple/local fees dynamic. Use Gaighat for district travel, Belaka for Koshi-side access, Katari for Triveni/western routes, and relevant rural-municipality roadheads for Rauta/Tapli/Limchungbung. Monsoon road and river conditions are dynamic.
