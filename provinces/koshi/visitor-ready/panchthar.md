# Panchthar — Visitor Ready (18/18)

## Introduction & history
Panchthar is a Limbu/Kirat cultural district of sacred hills, historic palaces, border ridges and highland trekking country. Phidim is the practical hub.

Labrekuti–Silauti = Kirat pilgrimage/history AREA; Mahaguru Phalgunanda Mausoleum = memorial POI within that cultural landscape. Sumhatlung, Kummayak–Kussayak and Sehonamlang = sacred hill/cave AREAS. Hilihang Palace = heritage AREA/viewpoint. Chiwabhanjyang = border-pass AREA; Phalot and Phokte Danda = high-ridge viewpoints/trek nodes. Timbu Pokhari = shared Panchthar–Taplejung alpine lake AREA with approach route, not a lake-centre pin. Sadhutar = hill/pond viewpoint AREA. Aagejung Monastery, Gumse Pathibhara, Gupteshwar Mahadev and Bhote Gadhi = monastery/religious/heritage POIs/areas. Battise Waterfall = waterfall access POI. Hile Pokhari and Jor Pokhari = wetland/lake AREAS.

Things: Kirat pilgrimage/cultural travel, high-ridge trekking, sunrise/mountain viewing, historic sites and wetlands.

No blanket district RAP; however cross-district itineraries entering Taplejung's specified restricted wards inherit Taplejung RAP requirements. Chiwabhanjyang is a border landscape and must not imply unrestricted international crossing. Access: Phidim → local roadheads for Silauti/Hilihang/Yangwarak/Phalelung; highland road/trail, fog, monsoon and border conditions dynamic.
