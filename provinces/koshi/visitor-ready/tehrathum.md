# Tehrathum — Visitor Ready (13/13)

## Introduction & history
Tehrathum is an eastern hill district strongly associated with Limbu culture and Nepal's great rhododendron belt. Basantapur is the principal tourism gateway to Tinjure–Milke–Jaljale.

Basantapur = hill-town/gateway AREA; Chotlung Park = Limbu cultural POI/AREA; Tinjure Danda = rhododendron ridge AREA; Tinjure–Milke–Jaljale = shared multi-district biodiversity/trekking CORRIDOR. Menchhayayem Danda = viewpoint/hiking AREA. Hyatrung Waterfall = waterfall with access node. Changlung Cave = cave POI with entrance/trailhead GIS. Marga Pokhari and Panchakanya Chhathar Pokhari = water/religious AREAS. Myanglung Bazaar, Morahang Bazaar and Sankranti Bazaar = historic settlement AREAS; Myanglung Cat Stone/Chichi Lung = cultural landmark POI.

Things to do: rhododendron-season hiking, Limbu cultural interpretation, waterfall/cave trips, hill-town travel and viewpoints.

No restricted-area permit applies. Use Dharan/Dhankuta/Hile approaches toward Basantapur for Tinjure; Myanglung is the central district hub; branch roads serve Phedap, Chhathar, Menchhayayem and Aathrai. Flower season, road conditions, waterfall flow and local opening/access are dynamic.
