# Taplejung — Visitor Ready (21/21)

## Introduction & history
Taplejung is Nepal's far-eastern high-Himalayan district, combining the Kanchenjunga massif, Limbu cultural landscapes, trans-Himalayan villages and Pathibhara pilgrimage. Historic movement followed the Tamor/Kanchenjunga valleys toward Tibet; today Phungling is the main service hub and Suketar a transport gateway.

## Main travel systems
**Pathibhara:** Pathibhara Devi Temple — sacred hill pilgrimage/viewpoint. Geometry: temple POINT + approach ROUTE. Things: pilgrimage, sunrise/mountain views. Access: Phungling/Suketar → current roadhead → final ascent on foot; verify current vehicle reach. No restricted-area permit for the normal Pathibhara visit; local/temple/transport charges dynamic.

**Kanchenjunga north:** Kanchenjunga Conservation Area (AREA), Lelep (gateway AREA), Ghunsa (village AREA), Lhonak Valley (CORRIDOR), Pang Pema/North Base Camp (trek objective AREA), Mount Kanchenjunga (mountain object), Olangchung Gola (historic village AREA), Diki Chhyoling Monastery (POINT after entrance GIS), Tipta La (border/pass ROUTE), Sele La (PASS/ROUTE). Things: multi-day trekking, Sherpa/Tibetan-border culture, monastery visits, alpine scenery. Access: Phungling → Taplethok/Lelep or current trailhead → staged trekking; Olangchung/Tipta La require border-sensitive routing.

**Kanchenjunga south:** Yalung Valley (CORRIDOR), Yalung Glacier (AREA), Oktang/South Base Camp (trek objective AREA), Tato Pani (local hot-spring POI; exact visitor pin HOLD until verified), Limbu Museum Mamangkhe (POINT after GIS), Sirijangha Cave (POINT/AREA after access verification).

**Eastern highlands:** Timbung Pokhari is a shared Taplejung–Panchthar alpine-lake landscape: WATER/AREA + approach ROUTES, never a single middle-of-lake pin. Fungfunge/Phungphunge Waterfall is a waterfall POI with trail/access node; Gumbadanda is a cultural-village AREA.

## Permits
Kanchenjunga Conservation Area entry and any current trekking requirements are separate from the Department of Immigration restricted-area permit. Immigration currently restricts Phaktanglung RM wards 6–7, Mikwakhola ward 5 and Sirijunga ward 8. Any route touching those wards must trigger current RAP verification through the official system. Mountaineering requires separate expedition authorization. Border crossing at Tipta La must never be implied from a trekking coordinate.

## Routing rule
Use Phungling as the district hub, Suketar as an air/road gateway when operational, and Lelep/Ghunsa/Mamangkhe as route-specific nodes. Weather, flights, landslides, roadheads, bridge conditions, accommodation and permit fees are dynamic.
