# Sunsari — Visitor Ready (18/18)

## Introduction & history
Sunsari joins Dharan's Vijayapur pilgrimage landscape with Chatara/Barahakshetra on the Koshi and the internationally important Koshi Tappu wetland system. Dharan and Itahari are the principal hubs.

## Dharan/Vijayapur
Vijayapur Hill = parent heritage/religious AREA; Budha Subba, Dantakali and Pindeshwar = individual temple precincts; Vijayapur Gadhi = fort AREA. Chinde Danda = hiking/viewpoint AREA; Panchakanya Forest = recreation/forest AREA. Route these as a Dharan heritage circuit, not four unrelated city pins.

## Koshi/Chatara
Chatara = river-town/gateway AREA; Chataradham and Barahakshetra Temple = separate pilgrimage precincts. Koshi Tappu Wildlife Reserve = shared Sunsari–Saptari–Udayapur protected AREA; Koshi Tappu Ramsar Site = wetland designation/landscape relationship, not a second reserve. Koshi Barrage = viewpoint/access AREA. Srilanka Tappu = dynamic river-island/wetland AREA whose shape/access can change with the river.

## Itahari/Terai wetlands
Tal Talaiya, Amaha/Amha Simsar and Mechikali Pokhari = water/recreation AREAS. Ramdhuni = temple/sacred-forest precinct.

## Permits/access
No restricted-area trekking permit. Wildlife-reserve entry and activity rules are separate and dynamic. Route wildlife visitors to a verified current reserve entrance/operator, never the reserve centroid. Dharan → Chatara/Barahakshetra for pilgrimage; Itahari is the highway hub; Koshi Barrage/reserve access depends on chosen sector and current river/road conditions.
