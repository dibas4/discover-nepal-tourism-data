# Dhankuta — Visitor Ready (26/26)

Dhankuta is an eastern-hill district of historic bazaars, community culture, rhododendron hills, wetlands and the Dharan–Bhedetar–Hile travel corridor.

Bhedetar is an AREA; Charles Point a viewpoint; Namaste Jharana a waterfall with an access node; Namje a cultural-village AREA; Namje–Thumki a community-tourism cluster; Thumki Hill a hiking/viewpoint AREA. Access is mainly from Dharan by the current hill road.

Dhankuta Bazaar, Hile Bazaar and Pakhribas Bazaar are town AREAS. Kopche Heritage Settlement is a historic neighbourhood and Kopche Heritage Trail is a walking ROUTE. Chuliban is a viewpoint. Pakhribas Agricultural Research Centre is an institutional/agro-tourism POI with visitor access to be checked. Pathibhara near Hile is a temple precinct. The district inventory's Aathpahariya cultural house and Syaule–Sipting community homestay remain cultural/community POIs with entrance-level GIS required.

Rajarani Valley is a landscape AREA; Rajarani Wetland and Rani Tal are nested wetland/lake AREAS rather than duplicates. Sadam is a rural nature area. Jitpur is a historic settlement. Leguwa–Arun is a river-confluence AREA; Nageshwar Temple, Latibhanjyang Shiva Statue, Banjhakri Park and Chintang Devi are separate religious/cultural POIs.

No restricted-area trekking permit applies district-wide. Waterfall, park, institution and homestay operations are dynamic. Main visitor routing: Dharan → Bhedetar → Dhankuta/Hile → Pakhribas, with branch roads for Rajarani, Namje, Jitpur and Leguwa. Towns/valleys use area centers; exact entrances only after GIS verification.
