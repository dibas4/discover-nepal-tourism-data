# Okhaldhunga — Visitor Ready (14/14)

## Introduction & history
Okhaldhunga is a middle-hill district of ridges, waterfalls, forts, pilgrimage sites and literary heritage, with Siddhicharan/Okhaldhunga Bazaar and Rumjatar as practical hubs.

## Places
Pokali Waterfall and Ratmate Waterfall = waterfall POIs with access-trail nodes, not generic municipality pins. Khiji Tholedemba and Patale = highland/viewpoint AREAS and hiking objectives. Champadevi Temple = sacred hill/temple precinct; Chandi Devi–Chandisthan Cave = temple/cave AREA; Libju = Kirat sacred landscape. Chisankhugadhi and Okhaldhunga Gadhi = heritage fort AREAS. Siddhicharan Shrestha birthplace and Srasta/Srijana Park = literary heritage POIs/areas. Rumjatar = settlement/gateway AREA. Tolu Gumba = monastery POI after exact entrance GIS. Prapcha Tourism Park = developing recreation site; publish facilities only after current verification.

## Things to do
Waterfall excursions, hill hiking, Himalayan viewpoints, fort/history visits, Kirat/Hindu pilgrimage, literary heritage and village travel.

## Permits & access
No district-wide restricted-area trekking permit applies. Temple/local entry and commercial charges are dynamic. From Kathmandu use current road/air-ground connections toward Okhaldhunga Bazaar/Rumjatar; branch to Likhu for Pokali, Khijidemba for Tholedemba, Champadevi for pilgrimage, and Chisankhugadhi for fort/waterfall travel. Road condition, monsoon landslides and final trail access must be checked before routing.
