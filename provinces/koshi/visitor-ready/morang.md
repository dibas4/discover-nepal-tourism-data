# Morang — Visitor Ready (17/17)

## Introduction & history
Morang combines Biratnagar's industrial/political heritage with Chure foothill viewpoints, Dhimal/Tharu cultural sites and a dense network of Terai wetlands. Biratnagar is the main urban hub.

Betana, Betini, Bhulke, Hasina, Jhalajhali and Lampate = separate wetland AREAS; Kanepokhari and Rajarani = lake/pond AREAS. Visitor activities: birdwatching, nature walks and boating only where currently authorized. Dhanpalgadhi = fort/religious heritage AREA. Birat Palace/Bhediyari Dhisko = archaeological AREA. Biratnagar Jute Mills = industrial-heritage facility/AREA; public entry must be verified before routing inside. Kali Mandir = temple POI. Jefale Danda = hill/viewpoint AREA reached through Kerabari approaches. Raniban = forest eco-tourism AREA. Interfaith Peace Park = cultural/religious park. Sunawarshi Pond = sacred/Indigenous cultural landscape. Baghjhora = forest/stream AREA.

No restricted-area trekking permit. Wetland fees, boating, institutional/industrial access and forest rules dynamic. Main routing: Biratnagar for heritage; east-west highway towns for Belbari/Urlabari/Pathari/Sundarharaicha wetlands; Kerabari for Jefale. Avoid wetland-centre pins—route to verified gates/parking/trailheads.
