# Sankhuwasabha — Visitor Ready (18/18)

## Introduction & history
Sankhuwasabha links the Arun river lowlands with Makalu-Barun's extreme Himalayan relief and old trade approaches toward Tibet. Khandbari/Tumlingtar form the practical visitor gateway; Chainpur is an important historic/craft centre.

## Places & activities
Makalu Barun National Park = protected AREA/POLYGON; Barun Valley and Arun Valley = CORRIDORS; Makalu Base Camp = high-altitude trek objective AREA; Mount Makalu = mountain/expedition object; Shipton La = PASS/ROUTE. Seduwa and Tashigaon are trekking settlement AREAS and access nodes. Things: Makalu Base Camp trek, biodiversity, Sherpa village culture, mountain photography.

Kimathanka = border-village AREA, not an international crossing promise; exact border movement rules dynamic. Tumlingtar = airport/gateway POINT/AREA; route onward through Khandbari to trail systems. Chhyankuti Danda = viewpoint AREA; Gupha Pokhari = lake/settlement AREA; Milke Danda and Tinjure–Milke–Jaljale = shared ridge/corridor systems. Sabha Pokhari = sacred alpine WATER/AREA with approach route. Chainpur Gadhi = heritage AREA; Chainpur Karuwa Park = craft/heritage POI; Hedangna Gadhi = heritage AREA with exact visitor access HOLD until verified.

## Permits & access
Makalu-Barun park entry is separate from restricted-area permission. Immigration currently restricts Bhotkhola RM wards 1–5 and Makalu RM ward 4; routes to Kimathanka or affected sectors must trigger current RAP verification. Climbing Makalu requires separate mountaineering authorization. Ordinary Chainpur/Khandbari/Tumlingtar visits do not inherit border RAP simply because they are in the district.

## How to visit
Primary long-distance gateway: flight/road to Tumlingtar → Khandbari. Makalu trekking continues toward Num/Seduwa/Tashigaon according to current roadhead and trail condition. Chainpur/Gupha Pokhari use eastern hill-road approaches. Never route to a park centroid, river midpoint or border line.
