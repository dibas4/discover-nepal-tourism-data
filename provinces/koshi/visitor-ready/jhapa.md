# Jhapa — Visitor Ready (23/23)

## Introduction & history
Jhapa is Koshi's southeastern gateway district, combining border access, tea country, wetlands, elephant movement landscapes, pilgrimage sites and Terai archaeological heritage. Kakarbhitta, Birtamod and Bhadrapur are practical hubs.

Kakarbhitta Border Gate = international gateway POINT/AREA with immigration/customs rules dynamic. Bahundangi Elephant Corridor = wildlife-landscape CORRIDOR; do not market as guaranteed elephant sighting or route visitors into conflict zones. Kanyam-style tea travel continues through Burney and Dhulabari tea-estate AREAS.

Arjundhara Jaleshwar, Kankai Dham/Kotihom, Satakshi Dham, Dhanushkoti Dham and Pau Pathibhara = pilgrimage precincts. Kichakbadh = archaeological/mythological heritage AREA. Kechana Kawal = geographical/wetland landscape; Kechana Jhil = nested water AREA, not duplicate. Jamunkhadi Simsar and Bhelagudi = wetland/recreation AREAS; Gauradaha/Birat Pokhari = historic pond AREAS. Domukha = riverbank recreation AREA. Mini Mountain and Dharagola = viewpoints. Salbari/Mahendra Park = recreation POIs/areas. Chillagadh = heritage AREA. Damak Tourism Park/Birat Buddha Village = developing project; publish only verified current facilities.

No restricted-area trekking permit. Border crossing requires normal immigration/customs compliance. Wetland boating, parks, temples and tea-estate visitor access are dynamic. Use Kakarbhitta for eastern entry, Birtamod for central Jhapa, Bhadrapur for southeast and Damak for west.
