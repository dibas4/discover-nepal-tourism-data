# Khotang — Visitor Ready (19/19)

## Introduction & history
Khotang is a Kirat cultural heartland and eastern-hill pilgrimage district. Diktel is the administrative/service hub; Halesi is the best-known visitor centre, where Hindu, Buddhist and Kirat sacred traditions overlap.

## Halesi system
Halesi Cave Complex = sacred cave AREA/precinct; Halesi Mahadev = temple/shrine POI inside that parent; Maratika = Buddhist identity/alias relationship within the wider sacred landscape, not a fake duplicate cave destination. Tuwachung–Jayajum = Kirat sacred hill AREA/viewpoint. Things: pilgrimage, cave visitation, cultural interpretation, hill views. Access: Kathmandu/Diktel approaches → Halesi town → walk to precinct entrances. Religious rules, festival crowd controls and offerings are dynamic; no restricted-area trekking permit.

## Other places
Baraha Pokhari = sacred lake WATER/AREA; Dhiplung and Lakuchumbu = waterfall POIs with access nodes; Majhuwagadhi, Mahuregadhi, Kepilasgadhi = fort/viewpoint AREAS; Rupakot Danda = hill/viewpoint AREA; Jantedhunga = historical/cultural hill AREA. Bhulbhule Dudhkunda Deudham, Indrani Pokhari, Paruhang–Sumnima Park, Sapsudhapa and Yalambar Park = religious/cultural sites. Kangsahop Lake = artificial-lake AREA with boating operations dynamic. Soldhunga = rock/viewpoint AREA.

## Access
Use Diktel for Majhuwagadhi/Rupakot and central sites; Halesi town for the sacred complex; municipality/rural-municipality centres for Baraha Pokhari and southern sites. Road times, monsoon access, local transport and exact trailheads remain dynamic. Exact pins for smaller local sites stay HOLD until entrance-level GIS is verified.
