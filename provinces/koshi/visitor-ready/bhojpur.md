# Bhojpur — Visitor Ready (30/30)

## Introduction & history
Bhojpur is known for Kirat cultural landscapes, traditional khukuri/metal craft, historic market towns and the highland Mundhum trekking country. Bhojpur Bazaar is the main district hub; Temkemaiyung and Salpasilichho form the principal trekking systems.

## Mundhum/highlands
Mundhum Trail = multi-district cultural ROUTE; Temke Danda, Maiyung Danda, Chakhewa, Tawa Bhanjyang, Rawa Dhap and Selme Danda = ridge/viewpoint/trail nodes. Salpa Pokhari = sacred WATER/AREA; Salpa Bhanjyang = pass; Silichung = mountain viewpoint/trek objective; Dobhane = trekking settlement. Hans Pokhari and Maluwa Pokhari = wetland/lake AREAS. Things: ridge trekking, Kirat culture, pilgrimage, bird/nature viewing.

## Heritage/culture
Bhojpur Bazaar and Taksar Bazaar = historic settlement AREAS; Bhojpur Khukuri Craft Centres = craft cluster, route users to verified workshops rather than one fake factory pin. Dingla Bazaar = historic market AREA. Hatuwagadhi Durbar = fort/palace heritage AREA; Siddhakali and Ramchandra temples = religious POIs/precincts. Arun River Corridor = river CORRIDOR with verified access nodes only.

## Local/emerging sites
Bidhya Pokhari, Chamere Gufa, Dimalung Park, Indreni Jharna, Mahavir Waterfall, Sawa Waterfall, Sahid Samadhi Sthal, Shila Sutkeri and Suntale Danda remain visitor objects but exact entrance/trailhead pins require GIS verification before navigation.

## Permits/access
No district-wide restricted-area permit. Trek/local charges dynamic. Reach Bhojpur Bazaar by current road/air-ground network; use Temkemaiyung approaches for Mundhum ridge nodes and Salpasilichho routes for Salpa/Silichung. Monsoon roads, accommodation and trail conditions are dynamic.
