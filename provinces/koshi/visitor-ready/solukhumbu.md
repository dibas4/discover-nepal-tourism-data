# Solukhumbu — Visitor Ready (34/34)

## Introduction & history
Solukhumbu combines the globally known Khumbu/Everest region with lower-Solu Sherpa settlements, monasteries and less-crowded trekking landscapes. Lukla/Namche serve Khumbu; Phaplu/Salleri serve lower Solu.

## Khumbu system
Sagarmatha National Park = UNESCO protected AREA/POLYGON. Lukla = air-gateway AREA/POINT; Namche Bazaar, Khumjung, Tengboche, Pangboche, Dingboche, Gorakshep and Thame = settlement AREAS with separate monastery POIs where applicable. Everest Base Camp and Kala Patthar = trekking objective AREAS/POINTS; Khumbu Glacier, Khumbu Icefall, Ngozumpa Glacier = natural AREAS not public-access pins. Gokyo Lakes = Ramsar lake-system AREA; Gokyo Ri = viewpoint route/object. Cho La, Kongma La, Renjo La = high-pass ROUTES. Everest, Ama Dablam = mountain/expedition objects. Island Peak = regulated climbing objective. Imja Tsho = glacial-lake AREA.

Things: EBC trek, Gokyo/Three Passes, monastery visits, Sherpa culture, viewpoints, regulated climbing. Never route ordinary trekkers onto Khumbu Icefall or mountain climbing lines.

## Lower Solu / Mahakulung
Pikey Peak = ridge/trek system; Junbesi = cultural village AREA; Thupten Chholing and Taksindu = monastery POIs/precincts; Dudhkunda Lake = sacred alpine lake + pilgrimage route. Hinku Valley, Mera Peak, Khiraule Monastery and Mahakulung Panch Pokhari form a separate eastern trekking system. Dharmadanda is a hill/viewpoint object.

## Permits
Sagarmatha National Park entry and local-area charges are separate from Immigration RAP. Current Immigration restriction applies specifically to Khumbu Pasang Lhamu RM ward 5; only itineraries entering that controlled ward trigger RAP. Climbing Everest/Ama Dablam/Island Peak/Mera requires the appropriate current climbing authorization. Flights, park/local fees, guide/TIMS rules and expedition royalties remain dynamic.

## Access
Kathmandu/Ramechhap → Lukla flights when scheduled, then trekking; road approaches can change. Lower Solu: Kathmandu → Salleri/Phaplu by road or flight when available. Mera/Hinku access can use Lukla or Mahakulung approaches depending itinerary. Route planner must check altitude acclimatization and current trail/flight/weather status.
