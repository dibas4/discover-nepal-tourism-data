# Ilam — Visitor Ready (37/37)

## Introduction & history
Ilam is Nepal's signature eastern tea-and-hill district, combining tea landscapes, Lepcha/Kirat heritage, Ramsar wetland, sunrise ridges and border-side trekking. Ilam Bazaar and Fikkal are the main visitor hubs.

## Tea/Suryodaya system
Kanyam Tea Garden and Panchakanya = tea-landscape AREAS; Ilam Tea Factory and Suryodaya Tea Tasting/Promotion Centre = facility POIs with current visitor access/hours dynamic. Shree Antu = rural-tourism AREA; Antu Danda = sunrise viewpoint; Antu Pokhari = water/recreation AREA. Fikkal = gateway AREA. Sanu Pathibhara = temple/viewpoint precinct. Pashupatinagar = border-town AREA, not an unrestricted India-crossing instruction. Rani Forest and Three-tier Waterfall are nature POIs/areas.

## Sandakpur/Mai Pokhari
Mai Pokhari = Ramsar wetland AREA; Sandakpur and Chhintapu = high-ridge trekking/viewpoint AREAS; Meghma and Jaubari = ridge settlements; route geometry should follow actual trail/road rather than straight lines. Patenagi = forest/viewpoint/red-panda habitat AREA with wildlife claims kept conservation-safe.

## Culture/nature
Fensong Gumba and Lepcha Museum = related but separate monastery/museum POIs. Gajurmukhi Dham, Mangmalung, Mai Beni, Kuinetar Omkareshwar = religious/natural precincts. Siddhithumka/Siddhi Thumka records must be deduplicated into one parent viewpoint landscape after GIS/name QA. Didi Bahini Jharana, Google Point, Gufathumki, Jhau Pokhari, Kuibhir, Mahabhir climbing area, Namsaling, Nayabazar, Ramite Danda and Tinkune Pokhari remain distinct visitor objects.

## Permits/access
No restricted-area trekking permit applies district-wide. Border formalities are separate. Commercial climbing, tea-factory visits, homestays and local fees are dynamic. Main access: Kakarbhitta/Birtamod → Fikkal/Kanyam/Shree Antu; continue to Ilam Bazaar or northern ridge roadheads. Fog, monsoon, tea-season access and trail conditions dynamic.
