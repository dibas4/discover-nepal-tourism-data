# Rolpa District — Visitor-Ready Tourism Enrichment

## Introduction
Rolpa is a rugged hill district centered on the Jaljala highlands, Kham Magar settlements, Guerrilla Trek history, former hill-state heritage and the Madi/Lungri river valleys.

## History
Rolpa's modern tourism story combines older Magar cultural landscapes and local principalities with nationally significant conflict-era history. Thabang, Jaljala and the Guerrilla Trek are the strongest visitor systems, while Libang is the practical district gateway.

## Main hubs
- **Libang** — district base and main services.
- **Thabang / Jelbang** — cultural and Jaljala trekking hubs.
- **Holeri** — southern gateway.

## Permit / access rule
No district-wide tourism permit. Trekking logistics, snow/weather, community products, museum operations and cave/lake access are dynamic.

## Recommended routing
Libang → Thabang/Jelbang → Jaljala for the main highland circuit; Holeri as southern access; Guerrilla Trek stages only with current lodging/road/trail information.
