# Dang District — Visitor-Ready Tourism Enrichment

## Introduction
Dang combines the broad Dang and Deukhuri valleys with major Hindu pilgrimage sites, lakes, caves, Tharu cultural landscapes, river corridors and historic hill settlements. Ghorahi, Tulsipur and Lamahi are the practical visitor hubs.

## History
Dang's identity reflects long settlement in the Inner Terai, former hill and valley power centres, Nath and Shaiva religious traditions, and living Tharu culture. The old Koilabas trade route connected the valley southward to the plains and India.

## Main hubs
- **Ghorahi** — central heritage and pilgrimage hub.
- **Tulsipur** — western valley and hill-access hub.
- **Lamahi** — Deukhuri and eastern corridor hub.

## Permit / access rule
Dang has no district-wide tourism permit. Border procedures, religious-event rules, lake operations, cave safety and archaeological access are separate dynamic fields.

## Recommended routing
Ghorahi pilgrimage circuit; Ghorahi → Barhakune; Lamahi → Jakhera; Tulsipur → Chhillikot after current road check; Rihar as a separate Deukhuri pilgrimage trip.
