# Nawalparasi (West) District — Visitor-Ready Tourism Enrichment

## Introduction
Nawalparasi West combines the Buddhist relic landscape of Ramgrama with Daunne ridge pilgrimage, Parasi's civic core and the changing Gandak/Susta border-river landscape.

## History
Ramgrama is associated with the ancient Koliya region and preserves a Buddhist relic stupa on Nepal's UNESCO Tentative List. The district also includes long-standing hill pilgrimage at Daunne and river-border communities shaped by the Gandak/Narayani system and modern irrigation infrastructure.

## Main visitor hubs
- **Parasi / Ramgram** — base for Ramgrama Stupa and nearby heritage.
- **Bardaghat** — base for Daunne ridge and northern hill sites.
- **Susta / Pratappur** — gateway for Gandak river-border landscapes; current access must be checked.

## Permit and entry model
No district-wide tourism permit applies. Ramgrama heritage rules, temple operations, forest access and especially Susta/Gandak border or infrastructure access must be checked as separate current conditions.

## Things-to-do routing
- **Buddhist pilgrimage:** Parasi → Ramgrama Stupa complex.
- **Hill pilgrimage/viewpoint:** Bardaghat → Daunne Devi → verified Daunne viewpoints.
- **River landscape:** only current legal Susta/Gandak public-access nodes.

## Safety / dynamic data
Gandak river channels, floods, border access, barrage security, Daunne road conditions and wetland water extent are highly dynamic. Never route users to river centroids, disputed crossings or unverified fort/wetland coordinates.
