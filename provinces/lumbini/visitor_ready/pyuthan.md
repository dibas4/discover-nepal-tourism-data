# Pyuthan District — Visitor-Ready Tourism Enrichment

## Introduction
Pyuthan is a hill district centered on the Jhimruk valley, Swargadwari pilgrimage, sacred springs, rivers, historic hill settlements and the Rolpa–Pyuthan highland edge.

## History
Swargadwari has long been the district's principal pilgrimage destination. Pyuthan Khalanga, Bijuwar and former hill settlements preserve administrative and local heritage, while the Jhimruk–Madi river system shapes both settlement and travel.

## Main hubs
- **Bijuwar / Pyuthan Khalanga** — central district services and heritage.
- **Bhingri** — primary road gateway for Swargadwari.

## Permit / access rule
No district-wide tourism permit. Swargadwari road/trail operations, temple events, river safety and community products are dynamic.

## Recommended routing
Bijuwar/Khalanga local heritage; Bhingri → Swargadwari; Gaumukhi as a separate longer rural trip; Lisne Lekh only with current trail/weather information.
