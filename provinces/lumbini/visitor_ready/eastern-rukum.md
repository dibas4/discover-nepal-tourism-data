# Eastern Rukum District — Visitor-Ready Tourism Enrichment

## Introduction
Eastern Rukum is a remote mountain district of Dhorpatan reserve landscapes, Putha and Sisne ranges, Rukumkot's sacred lake basin, Kham Magar villages, high pastures and historical trekking corridors.

## History
The district combines older Kham Magar settlement and transhumance traditions with modern protected-area management around Dhorpatan and newer political-administrative identity after the Rukum split. Rukumkot remains the main accessible cultural centre.

## Main hubs
- **Rukumkot** — district base for lake, temples and local services.
- **Taksera / Maikot / Hukam** — northern cultural and trekking hubs; road conditions dynamic.
- **Dhorpatan-side approaches** — reserve and highland access; use current legal routes.

## Permit / access rule
Protected-area entry for Dhorpatan and any climbing/expedition authorization are separate from ordinary district travel. Hunting permits are a specialized regulated activity and must never be implied by a normal visitor guide.

## Recommended routing
Rukumkot local circuit; multi-day cultural travel to Taksera/Maikot only with current road and accommodation checks; Dhorpatan/Putha routes only with verified reserve/trek logistics.
