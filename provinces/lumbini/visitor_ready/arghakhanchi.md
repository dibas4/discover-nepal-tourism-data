# Arghakhanchi District — Visitor-Ready Tourism Enrichment

## Introduction
Arghakhanchi is a middle-hill district of pilgrimage shrines, former hill principalities, forested ridges, river valleys and rural settlements. Sandhikharka is the practical base for most visits.

## History
The district name reflects the historic Argha and Khanchi principalities. Their hill forts, temples and settlements remain central to local identity, while Panini Tapobhumi and Supa Deurali add major religious-cultural importance.

## Main hubs
- **Sandhikharka** — main district base.
- **Thada** — southern Sitganga gateway.

## Permit / access rule
No district-wide tourism permit. Temple etiquette, forest access, cave safety, road conditions and local recreation facilities are separate dynamic fields.

## Recommended routing
Sandhikharka → Supa Deurali; Sandhikharka → Argha/Khanchi heritage; Sandhikharka → Narapani; longer southern trip toward Thada/Sitganga only with current road checks.
