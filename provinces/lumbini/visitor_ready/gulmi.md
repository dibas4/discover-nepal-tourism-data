# Gulmi District — Visitor-Ready Tourism Enrichment

## Introduction
Gulmi is a middle-hill district of sacred ridges, Kali Gandaki pilgrimage, former hill principalities, caves, market towns and agro-tourism. Tamghas is the main base; Ridi/Ruru is the major pilgrimage gateway.

## History
Gulmi's heritage includes the sacred Resunga landscape, the historic Ridi/Ruru pilgrimage corridor, and former principalities such as Dhurkot and Isma. Traditional markets, coffee cultivation and orange farming add living cultural and agricultural identity.

## Main hubs
- **Tamghas / Resunga Municipality** — district base.
- **Ridi / Rurukshetra** — Kali Gandaki pilgrimage hub.
- **Musikot / Wami** — eastern-northern heritage gateway.

## Permit / access rule
No district-wide tourism permit. Religious sites, caves, forests, farms and river ghats use site-specific or seasonal access conditions.

## Recommended routing
Tamghas → Resunga; Tamghas → Dhurkot/Isma heritage; separate Ridi/Ruru pilgrimage trip; agro-tourism only through confirmed hosts.
