# Banke District — Visitor-Ready Tourism Enrichment

## Introduction
Banke combines Nepalgunj's border-city culture with Banke National Park, Rapti river landscapes, Tharu community tourism and the practical transport gateway of Kohalpur.

## History
Nepalgunj developed as a major western Terai market and Nepal–India trade gateway, while the northern Chure and sal forests became part of Nepal's modern protected-area network through Banke National Park.

## Main hubs
- **Nepalgunj** — city, pilgrimage and border-culture hub.
- **Kohalpur** — highway and park-access hub.
- **Rapti Sonari / Agaiya side** — landscape and park-approach zone; use only verified gates/access nodes.

## Permit / access rule
Banke National Park entry is separate from ordinary district travel. Border rules, park fees, safari availability, forest permissions and community-tourism operation must be refreshed before publication.

## Recommended routing
Nepalgunj city heritage/pilgrimage; Nepalgunj → Kohalpur → verified Banke NP gate; Kohalpur → Gavar Valley only when an active community product is confirmed.
