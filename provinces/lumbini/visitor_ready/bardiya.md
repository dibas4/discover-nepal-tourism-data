# Bardiya District — Visitor-Ready Tourism Enrichment

## Introduction
Bardiya is one of Nepal's strongest wildlife districts, centered on Bardiya National Park, Thakurdwara, the Karnali–Geruwa river system, Khata wildlife corridor, blackbuck habitat and Tharu community tourism.

## History
The district's tourism identity is shaped by protected-area conservation, Tharu settlement landscapes and river systems that connect the western Terai with the Karnali basin. Thakurdwara became the primary visitor gateway to Bardiya National Park, while Rajapur and Gulariya remain key settlement hubs.

## Main hubs
- **Thakurdwara** — primary wildlife and lodge hub.
- **Gulariya** — district headquarters and blackbuck/pilgrimage hub.
- **Rajapur / Geruwa** — river landscape and western wetland hub.

## Permit / access rule
Bardiya National Park and Blackbuck Conservation Area use protected-area entry rules. Commercial rafting, jungle walks, safaris and homestays are separate current operations.

## Recommended routing
Thakurdwara wildlife stay; Gulariya → Khairapur blackbuck; Rajapur/Geruwa river landscape; active community products only after direct status confirmation.

## Safety / dynamic data
Wildlife movement, river levels, dolphin sighting zones, park closures, safari availability, homestay operation and wetland water levels are dynamic.
