## Priority source and verification notes

- Jaljala is Rolpa's flagship natural and trekking landscape and should be split into access, ecology, pilgrimage and trekking records in the future CMS.
- Thabang and the Guerrilla Trek are nationally significant but require careful, factual treatment of conflict history.
- Gajulkot is a distinct historical asset and should not be reduced to a generic viewpoint.
- Several lakes, caves and shrines come from district heritage inventories; they remain provisional until mapped and ground-checked.
- Lisne Lekh is cross-district with Pyuthan. Maintain separate access records but one shared parent destination.
