## Priority source and verification notes

- Pyuthan's core geography is the Jhimruk valley, with the Madi–Jhimruk river system and high northern watershed.
- Swargadwari is the clear flagship destination and should receive a dedicated cluster page rather than a single generic record.
- Heritage inventories list many small temples and kot sites; only individually identifiable places are included here.
- Lisne Lekh is cross-district with Rolpa. Keep separate district access records rather than duplicating it as two unrelated attractions.
