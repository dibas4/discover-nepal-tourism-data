## Priority clusters

1. **Rukumkot and Rukmini Lake** — strongest accessible lake, religious and settlement cluster.
2. **Putha–Dhorpatan–Uttar Ganga** — mountain, reserve, trekking and pastoral landscape.
3. **Taksera–Maikot–Hukam** — Kham Magar culture and community-tourism corridor.
4. **Sisne Himal and Bhume** — mountain views, cultural villages and Guerrilla Trek history.
