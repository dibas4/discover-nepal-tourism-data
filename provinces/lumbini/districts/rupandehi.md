# Rupandehi District — Visitor-Ready Tourism Enrichment

## Introduction
Rupandehi combines the UNESCO World Heritage birthplace landscape of Lumbini with Devdaha, ancient settlement archaeology, Butwal's fort and civic heritage, lakes, pilgrimage corridors and modern transport gateways.

## History
Lumbini preserves the archaeological and sacred landscape associated with the birth of Siddhartha Gautama. The modern Lumbini Master Plan organizes the Sacred Garden, monastic zones, cultural centre and New Lumbini Village. East of Lumbini, Devdaha is associated with the Koliya kingdom and Maya Devi's maternal landscape. Butwal preserves later Sen- and Anglo-Nepal War-era heritage.

## Main visitor hubs
- **Lumbini** — primary heritage/pilgrimage hub.
- **Bhairahawa / Siddharthanagar** — airport, border and hotel gateway for Lumbini.
- **Butwal** — hub for Jitgadhi, Siddha Baba corridor and northern Rupandehi.
- **Devdaha** — hub for the eastern Buddhist-heritage landscape.

## Permit and entry model
Rupandehi has no district-wide travel permit. UNESCO/Lumbini site tickets, museum opening hours, individual monastery access, boating operations and religious-site etiquette are separate operational fields and must be refreshed before publication.

## Things-to-do routing
- **Buddhist pilgrimage:** Lumbini Sacred Garden → monastic zones → World Peace Pagoda; extend to Devdaha with a road transfer.
- **Archaeology:** Maya Devi/ruins → Devdaha/Bhawanipur/Kanyamai → Sainamaina.
- **History:** Jitgadhi + Butwal heritage.
- **Nature:** authorized Crane Sanctuary access and Gajedi lakeside.

## Safety / dynamic data
Festival crowds, monastery closures, Lumbini transport services, Tinau monsoon conditions, lake boating and Siddha Baba highway access are dynamic. Route to entrances and shoreline nodes, never to water or heritage-area centroids.
