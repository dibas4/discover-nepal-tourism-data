# Salyan — Visitor-Ready Tourism Enrichment

## Introduction and history
Salyan is a mid-hill district of lakes, ridges, river valleys, pilgrimage sites and farming landscapes. **Salyan Khalanga** is the main hub, with Kapurkot, Bagchaur and Bangad Kupinde acting as sub-hubs.

## Permit/access profile
No district-wide restricted-area permit is assigned. Lake boating, minor waterfalls, farm visits, river conditions and ridge trails are dynamic.

## Planner defaults
Use Khalanga for central trips, Bangad Kupinde for Kupinde Lake and Kapurkot/Bagchaur for eastern/southern approaches. Waterfalls and farm visits stay unpublished until exact access and host/operator status are verified.
