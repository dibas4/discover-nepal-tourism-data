# Jumla — Visitor-Ready Tourism Enrichment

## Introduction and history
Jumla combines the Sinja Valley Khasa heritage landscape, Chandannath pilgrimage traditions, Tila/Hima river valleys, Marsi rice and apple agriculture, hot springs and highland trekking. **Jumla Khalanga** is the principal hub.

## Permit/access profile
No district-wide restricted-area permit is assigned. Rara/Dolpo cross-district treks inherit the rules of any protected or restricted geography they enter. Flights, highland roads, trail conditions and farm access are dynamic.

## Planner defaults
Use Khalanga for district access and Sinja/Kanakasundari as a western heritage cluster. Never expose private farms, inscription panels, pasture collection sites or unverified ridge points as generic public POIs.
