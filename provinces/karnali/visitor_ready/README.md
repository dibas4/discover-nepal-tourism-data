## Province-wide parent systems
1. **Shey Phoksundo National Park** — protected-area parent centered in Dolpa and historically extending into Mugu; current official boundaries must follow DNPWC, not old static maps.
2. **Rara National Park** — protected-area parent in Mugu with a portion in Jumla; Rara Lake is a water polygon and visitors route to verified shoreline/park access.
3. **Chhayanath National Park** — current DNPWC protected-area record in Mugu; keep separate from Rara and Shey Phoksundo.
4. **Upper Dolpa restricted area** — Immigration permit system applying only to covered wards, separate from park entry.
5. **Lower Dolpa restricted area** — separate Immigration permit geography and fee system.
6. **Humla restricted areas** — Immigration permit system for specified Simikot/Namkha/Changkheli wards, not a district-wide permit.
7. **Mugum Karmarong restricted area** — Immigration permit system for covered northern Mugu geography.
8. **Mahabu / Mahawai Lekh** — one Dailekh–Kalikot ridge and pilgrimage landscape with district-side approaches.
9. **Jumla–Rara corridor** — one cross-district trekking system linking Jumla and Mugu.
10. **Jajarkot–Dolpa corridor** — remote access/trekking network; road, bridge and trail state must be dated.
11. **Guerrilla Trek / Sisne–Jaljala connections** — Western Rukum cross-district route systems linked to Eastern Rukum and Rolpa.
12. **Bheri, Karnali, Tila, Humla Karnali and Mugu Karnali** — river corridors use line/area geometry and named safe access nodes, never midpoint pins.

## Dynamic-data policy
Never hard-code as timeless: restricted-area fees, national-park entry fees, flight schedules, helicopter service, border openings, road/bridge condition, trail closures, accommodation, rafting operators, lake boating, festival dates, high-pass safety, snow conditions, checkpoint operations, pasture/medicinal-herb access or active community tourism.

## Routing safety rules
- Lakes route to verified shoreline/public entrances, never lake centroids.
- Parks route to official gates/checkpoints, never protected-area centroids.
- Mountain passes and long treks are route nodes/route networks, not standalone navigation points.
- Archaeological remains and inscriptions stay AREA/HOLD until authoritative monument coordinates are available.
- Wildlife/habitat records never imply guaranteed sightings.
- Border routes, sacred highlands and restricted areas require a current legal-access check before traveler-facing routing.
- Community pasture, medicinal-herb, farm and living-culture records require consent before exposing specific private/sensitive locations.

## Source backbone
Department of Immigration for restricted-area permits; Department of National Parks and Wildlife Conservation for protected areas; Department of Archaeology for Sinja, Bhurti, Dullu and related monument data; Nepal Tourism Board and Karnali/local governments for visitor access; municipality/community verification for local facilities and exact public access points.
