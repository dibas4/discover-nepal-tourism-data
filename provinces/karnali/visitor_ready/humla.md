# Humla — Visitor-Ready Tourism Enrichment

## Introduction
Humla is a remote Himalayan border district centered on Simikot, the Humla Karnali valley, the historic Simikot–Hilsa route, Limi Valley and trans-Himalayan Buddhist settlements. **Simikot** is the primary travel hub.

## History and cultural context
Humla preserves ancient trade and pilgrimage links with Tibet, Tibetan Buddhist village culture in Namkha, and Hindu pilgrimage traditions along the Karnali. Border, aviation and restricted-area access are operationally sensitive.

## Permit and access profile
- Restricted-area permit applies only to the current Immigration-defined Humla wards, not the entire district.
- Simikot–Hilsa/Limi/Kailash-related travel must use current border and Immigration rules.
- Flights, helicopters, road openings, passes, bridges and accommodation are dynamic.

## Planner defaults
Use Simikot as the district hub. Never generate Hilsa, Limi or Lapcha itineraries without a current permit/border check; never route to a river centroid or approximate high-pass coordinate when the current trail is unknown.
