# Surkhet — Visitor-Ready Tourism Enrichment

## Introduction and history
Surkhet is Karnali's main gateway district, centered on Birendranagar and the Surkhet Valley. It combines Kakrebihar archaeology, Bulbule Lake, Deuti Bajai, Mangalgadhi, urban viewpoints, Bheri/Karnali river corridors and rural valleys. **Birendranagar** is the principal province and district hub.

## Permit/access profile
No district-wide restricted-area permit is assigned. Archaeological limits, rafting operations, lake boating, airport access, road conditions and living-culture visits are dynamic.

## Planner defaults
Use Birendranagar for nearly all district travel. Kakrebihar routes to the protected monument precinct, Bulbule routes to public shoreline/park entrances, and river adventure routes require a current operator and flow check.
