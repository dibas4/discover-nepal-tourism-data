# Kalikot — Visitor-Ready Tourism Enrichment

## Introduction and history
Kalikot combines Karnali and Tila river corridors, the Raskot valley, Mahabu/Mahawai ridge, Badimalika approaches, rural Khasa settlements and the dramatic Karnali Highway. **Manma/Khadachakra** is the main access hub.

## Permit/access profile
No district-wide restricted-area permit is assigned. Cross-district routes such as Badimalika and Mahabu inherit the rules of the destination they enter. Highway, airport, trail and river conditions are dynamic.

## Planner defaults
Use Manma as the district hub and Raskot as the main western valley sub-hub. Never route to an airport, waterfall, deval or confluence until the exact public access point and current condition are verified.
