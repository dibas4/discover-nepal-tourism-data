# Mugu — Visitor-Ready Tourism Enrichment

## Introduction and history
Mugu is centered on Rara Lake and Rara National Park, Gamgadhi/Talcha access, Chhayanath pilgrimage, Mugum Karmarong's trans-Himalayan villages and northern trade-route landscapes. **Gamgadhi** and **Talcha** are the principal practical hubs.

## Permit/access profile
- Restricted-area permit applies only to the current Immigration-defined Mugum Karmarong geography, not the entire district.
- Rara National Park entry is separate from restricted-area permits.
- Chhayanath National Park is now an official DNPWC protected area and must be treated as a separate protected-area system where applicable.
- Flights, roads, lake access, trail conditions and border access are dynamic.

## Planner defaults
Use Gamgadhi/Talcha for general district access and route Rara visitors to verified park and shoreline access. Northern Mugum Karmarong itineraries must always trigger a current restricted-area permit and border-access check.
