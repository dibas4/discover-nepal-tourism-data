# Jajarkot — Visitor-Ready Tourism Enrichment

## Introduction and history
Jajarkot combines the historic Khalanga royal landscape, Bheri/Nalgad river corridors, highlands of Barekot and Kushe, rural Khas/Magar culture and trekking approaches toward Dolpa and Rukum. **Jajarkot Khalanga** is the main district hub. Heritage content must be rechecked after the 2023 earthquake.

## Permit/access profile
No district-wide restricted-area permit is assigned. Roads, bridges, river access, earthquake-affected buildings and remote trekking logistics are dynamic. High pastures require community approval.

## Planner defaults
Use Khalanga for normal access. Do not route travelers into earthquake-damaged structures, unverified waterfalls, pasture collection areas or river centroids.
