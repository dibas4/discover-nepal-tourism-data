# Western Rukum — Visitor-Ready Tourism Enrichment

## Introduction and history
Western Rukum combines Musikot/Khalanga heritage, Syarpu Lake, Chaurjahari Valley, Bheri and Sani Bheri corridors, conflict-history landscapes and cross-district trekking connections toward Eastern Rukum and Rolpa. **Musikot Khalanga** is the main hub, with **Chaurjahari** as the southern gateway.

## Permit/access profile
No district-wide restricted-area permit is assigned. Flights, lake boating, bridges, conflict-memory sites, cross-district treks and remote village services are dynamic.

## Planner defaults
Use Musikot for central/northern trips and Chaurjahari for southern access. Syarpu routes only to verified shoreline access; conflict-history sites require factual, locally reviewed interpretation; Guerrilla Trek and Sisne–Jaljala routes stay cross-district entities.
