# Dailekh — Visitor-Ready Tourism Enrichment

## Introduction
Dailekh combines medieval Khasa heritage, the Dullu–Panchakoshi sacred landscape, natural-gas flame traditions, archaeological monuments, hill viewpoints and Karnali river-valley travel. The principal visitor hubs are **Dailekh Bazaar** and **Dullu**.

## History
The district preserves important evidence of the medieval Khasa cultural world. Dullu, inscriptions, deval monuments and the Panchakoshi pilgrimage landscape should be interpreted as connected heritage rather than isolated attractions.

## Permit and access profile
- No district-wide restricted-area trekking permit is assigned.
- Archaeological/religious site entry, local festival access and any monument fees are dynamic.
- Mahabu is a shared Dailekh–Kalikot ridge; roads/trails are weather-sensitive.
- Rivers use corridor geometry and safe named access nodes, never a river-centroid pin.

## Planner defaults
Use **Dailekh Bazaar** for general district access and **Dullu** for the heritage/pilgrimage cluster. Never send a traveler to an approximate natural-gas source, archaeological centroid, river midpoint or unverified shrine coordinate.
