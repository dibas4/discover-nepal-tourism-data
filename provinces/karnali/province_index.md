## Verification model

- **Government-managed / protected:** protected areas and officially managed heritage supported by authoritative records.
- **Established:** widely recognized destination; coordinates, wards, permits or current access may still be pending.
- **First-round researched:** relevant inventory record requiring stronger municipality-level or specialist confirmation.
- **Needs local verification:** changing natural feature, seasonal route, community facility, small structure or uncertain mapped location.

## Karnali-specific data cautions

- Remote trail, flight, road, bridge, permit and accommodation information must be date-stamped.
- Village and monastery names often have multiple English spellings; preserve aliases but select an official preferred name only after local verification.
- Mountain passes, valleys, protected areas and trekking regions are geographic entities, not single-point attractions.
- Wildlife records represent habitat and conservation value, not guaranteed sighting locations.
- Seasonal pilgrimage, yarsagumba collection and high-pasture access require community approval and current safety checks.
