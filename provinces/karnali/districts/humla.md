## Cross-border and cross-entity relationships

- Simikot, Hilsa and the Simikot–Hilsa trail are separate entities connected by the Kailash pilgrimage and historic trade route.
- Limi Valley is a broad cultural landscape containing Halji, Til and Jang/Dzang villages, Rinchenling Monastery, high pastures and access passes.
- Lapcha La is a Nepal-side viewpoint and access landscape; Mount Kailash and Manasarovar themselves are outside Nepal.
- Humla Karnali is a linear river corridor and must not be represented as one attraction coordinate.

## Priority verification tasks

1. Confirm municipalities, wards and preferred English spellings for monasteries and villages.
2. Obtain exact coordinates for temples, monasteries, hot springs, trailheads and viewpoint access points.
3. Record restricted-area permit rules and border requirements with effective dates.
4. Verify current flight, helicopter, road, bridge, accommodation and emergency-service status.
5. Add recent image evidence only after location identity and usage rights are confirmed.
6. Separate public heritage sites from private lodges, temporary camps and tour-operated facilities.

## Source direction

Use Government of Nepal, district and rural-municipality records, immigration/permit notices, Nepal Tourism Board material, peer-reviewed conservation research and locally verified monastery or community records. General trekking descriptions are discovery leads, not final proof.
