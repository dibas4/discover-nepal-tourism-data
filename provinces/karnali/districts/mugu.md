## Source and verification notes

- Rara Lake and Rara National Park are the strongest government-managed anchors for the district.
- Gamgadhi is the district headquarters and Talcha is the principal air gateway for Rara travel.
- Northern Mugum Karmarong records represent remote geographic and cultural landscapes; village spellings, wards, monasteries, routes and access need municipality or field confirmation.
- A newly reported Chhayanath protected-area designation appeared in 2025 sources. Its legal boundary, management status and relationship to Shey Phoksundo must be confirmed from an authoritative government notice before publication as a finalized protected-area record.
- Roads, flights, trails, bridges, permits, border access, accommodation, lake boating and seasonal services must always carry a last-verified date.
