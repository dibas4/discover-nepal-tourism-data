## Key source and identity notes

- Sinja Valley is a UNESCO Tentative List cultural site, not an inscribed World Heritage Site.
- Chandannath and Bhairavnath are related but distinct components of one religious complex.
- Marsi rice and apple records describe working cultural landscapes; individual farms should not be exposed without permission.
- Cross-district trekking corridors require relationship records rather than duplicate, disconnected attractions.

## Phase-two verification priorities

1. Obtain Department of Archaeology boundaries and coordinates for Sinja remains, caves and inscription locations.
2. Confirm municipalities, wards and exact coordinates for Kanakasundari, Tatopani and highland trailheads.
3. Record current road, flight, bridge, lodging and trekking conditions with verification dates.
4. Verify community permission, grazing seasons and conservation restrictions for meadows, herb landscapes and remote routes.
5. Build aliases for Sinja/Sinjaa, Khalanga/Jumla Bazaar and local village spellings.
