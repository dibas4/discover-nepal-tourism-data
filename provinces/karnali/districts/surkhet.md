## Research and verification notes

- Kakrebihar monument components, archaeological limits, and protected boundaries require Department of Archaeology or specialist verification before exact coordinates are published.
- Bulbule Lake water level, expansion works, boating, ticketing, and park maintenance are time-sensitive.
- Bheri rafting records require verified operators, seasonal flow, safety standards, and exact access points.
- Raji cultural records must be developed with community consent and should not reduce living culture to a staged attraction.
- River corridors, valleys, forests, and highways are geographic entities and should later receive geometry or relationship records rather than one point coordinate.
