## Quality and verification notes

- This file contains **32 first-round records**.
- Protected-area, Ramsar and major district facts are supported by Nepal's Department of National Parks and Wildlife Conservation records and government-linked district information.
- Remote monasteries, passes, village spellings and trail geometry require municipality, park-office or specialist verification.
- Flights, bridges, checkpoints, accommodation, permits, weather and seasonal access must be stored as separately dated operational data.
- Wildlife habitat records must never be presented as guaranteed sighting locations.
- Coordinates have deliberately not been guessed.
