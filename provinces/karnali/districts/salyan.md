## Verification priorities

- Confirm current visitor access, boating, shoreline safety, and facilities at Kupinde Lake.
- Confirm official names, wards, coordinates, and festival calendars for Khairabang Bhagwati and Chhatreshwari temples.
- Treat Sharada, Bheri, Babai headwaters, valleys, ridges, and trail corridors as geographic entities rather than single-point attractions.
- Field-check minor waterfalls, ridge viewpoints, community homestays, and agro-tourism operators before publication.
- Preserve alternate spellings for local place names until municipalities or local communities confirm a preferred English form.
