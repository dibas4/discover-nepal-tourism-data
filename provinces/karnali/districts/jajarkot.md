## Verification priorities

1. Confirm the current physical condition and legal status of Jajarkot Durbar and other earthquake-affected structures.
2. Obtain municipality-approved coordinates for waterfalls, temples, viewpoints and heritage settlements.
3. Date-stamp all road, bridge, accommodation and trekking-access information.
4. Treat Barekot, Kushe, river corridors and high pastures as geographic entities, not single-point attractions.
5. Get community consent before publishing medicinal-herb, pasture or culturally sensitive locations.
