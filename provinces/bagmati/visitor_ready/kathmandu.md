# Kathmandu District — Visitor-Ready Tourism Enrichment

## Introduction
Kathmandu District is the historic and contemporary core of Nepal's capital region. Its visitor landscape combines living Newar urban heritage, Hindu and Buddhist pilgrimage, royal and national museums, traditional settlements, forested national-park ridges, lakes and short hikes. The district should be mapped as connected visitor areas rather than as one Kathmandu pin.

## Short history
Kathmandu's tourism landscape reflects several historical layers: ancient and medieval Hindu-Buddhist sacred sites; Newar city and trading culture; the Malla royal era centered on palace squares; the later Shah state and royal institutions; and modern conservation of the northern valley rim. Kathmandu Durbar Square, Swayambhunath, Boudhanath and Pashupati are components of the UNESCO Kathmandu Valley World Heritage property.

## Main visitor hubs
- **Central Kathmandu / Thamel** — practical urban tourism hub for Durbar Square, museums, Swayambhu and onward taxis.
- **Boudha / Gaushala** — practical hub for Boudhanath, Pashupati and Kopan.
- **Budhanilkantha** — northern hub for Budhanilkantha Temple and Shivapuri hikes.
- **Sundarijal** — eastern trailhead for Shivapuri and Bagmati-source routes.
- **Kirtipur** — southwest heritage hub for Kirtipur and Taudaha.
- **Pharping / Dakshinkali** — southern pilgrimage and monastery-circuit hub.
- **Thankot** — western transport hub for Chandragiri.

## Map rules
- Urban districts, towns, heritage quarters and religious precincts use **area geometry**.
- Individual temples, stupas, museums and cable-car stations use **point geometry** when a reliable coordinate is available.
- National park sectors use **polygon/area geometry** and route visitors to actual gates or trailheads.
- Hiking routes use **route/corridor geometry**.
- Dynamic prices, opening hours, transport schedules and trail conditions must be checked before traveler publication.

## Places to visit

### 1. Kathmandu Durbar Square / Hanuman Dhoka
**Type:** heritage precinct / palace complex  
**Geometry:** area; UNESCO reference coordinate **27.703889, 85.308333**. Do not treat the coordinate as every building in the square.  
**Introduction:** Kathmandu's historic royal square contains Hanuman Dhoka palace courtyards, temples, Kumari Ghar and dense living-city heritage around Basantapur.  
**Things to do:** heritage walk, palace/museum visit where open, architecture, Kumari-house exterior, photography where permitted, explore nearby old-city streets.  
**Permit / entry:** no travel permit. Heritage-site entrance fee applies to relevant foreign visitors; current fee must remain dynamic.  
**How to visit:** from Thamel/central Kathmandu, walk or take a short taxi/rickshaw toward Basantapur/New Road and enter through the public heritage-zone access. The planner should route to an entrance edge, not the center of the square.  
**Sources:** UNESCO Kathmandu Valley maps; Kathmandu Metropolitan City; Nepal Tourism Board.

### 2. Kirtipur heritage town
**Type:** historic town / living cultural area  
**Geometry:** area centered approximately **27.666667, 85.283333**; use a town boundary/radius rather than one attraction pin.  
**Introduction:** Historic Newar hill town southwest of central Kathmandu, with traditional streets, temples, stupas and viewpoints.  
**Things to do:** old-town walk, Newar food, Bagh Bhairav, Chilancho, Uma-Maheshwor and other heritage stops, valley views.  
**Permit / entry:** no town-wide tourism permit identified; individual sites may have customs or local rules.  
**How to visit:** taxi/bus from central Kathmandu to Kirtipur, then explore the compact heritage core mainly on foot.  
**Source:** Nepal Tourism Board and Kirtipur Municipality.

### 3. Swayambhunath
**Type:** Buddhist-Hindu sacred complex / UNESCO component  
**Geometry:** sacred-hill area with stupa reference point **27.714444, 85.292500**.  
**Introduction:** Ancient hilltop stupa complex west of Kathmandu, surrounded by shrines and monasteries and overlooking the urban valley.  
**Things to do:** circumambulation, monastery/shrine exploration, city views, religious-art observation.  
**Permit / entry:** no travel permit; heritage entrance fee applies to relevant visitors and should be stored dynamically.  
**How to visit:** short taxi from Thamel/central Kathmandu to the eastern stair approach or western vehicle-access side; final exploration is on foot.  
**Sources:** UNESCO; Nepal Tourism Board.

### 4. Boudhanath Stupa
**Type:** Buddhist stupa precinct / UNESCO component  
**Geometry:** area around reference point **27.721111, 85.364167**.  
**Introduction:** Major Tibetan Buddhist pilgrimage center surrounded by monasteries, shops and rooftop viewpoints, historically connected to trans-Himalayan trade routes.  
**Things to do:** clockwise kora, monastery visits where permitted, observe rituals, rooftop views, Tibetan-Himalayan food and crafts.  
**Permit / entry:** no travel permit; heritage-zone entrance fee for relevant visitors is dynamic.  
**How to visit:** taxi or public transport from central Kathmandu to Boudha; walk from the main road into the pedestrian stupa precinct.  
**Sources:** UNESCO; Nepal Tourism Board; Kathmandu heritage sources.

### 5. Pashupatinath Temple / Pashupati area
**Type:** Hindu sacred landscape / UNESCO component  
**Geometry:** large religious precinct along Bagmati River around UNESCO reference **27.710523, 85.348840**.  
**Introduction:** Nepal's principal Shiva pilgrimage landscape, containing the main Pashupatinath temple, ghats, shrines and ashrams along the Bagmati River.  
**Things to do:** pilgrimage, observe temple architecture and riverside ritual landscape, visit permitted public areas, evening religious atmosphere when accessible.  
**Permit / entry:** no travel permit; tourist entry rules/fees are dynamic. **Main temple access is restricted to Hindus**; non-Hindu visitors use permitted exterior/riverbank viewpoints.  
**How to visit:** taxi/public transport from Thamel or Boudha to Gaushala/Pashupati approaches, then walk through the precinct. Route users to a public entrance, not directly to the sanctum.  
**Sources:** UNESCO; Nepal Tourism Board; Pashupati Area Development Trust.

### 6. Budhanilkantha Temple / Jal Narayan
**Type:** Hindu temple  
**Geometry:** point/precinct around **27.778056, 85.362222**.  
**Introduction:** Vishnu shrine at the foot of Shivapuri centered on the famous reclining stone image of Narayan.  
**Things to do:** temple visit, pilgrimage, combine with northern Kathmandu/forest excursions.  
**Permit / entry:** no separate tourism permit identified; religious etiquette applies and any current local fee should be checked.  
**How to visit:** taxi/bus north from central Kathmandu to Budhanilkantha; final approach is a short walk through the temple area.  
**Sources:** Nepal Tourism Board; Budhanilkantha Municipality.

### 7. Kopan (Kapan) Monastery
**Type:** Tibetan Buddhist monastery / study center  
**Geometry:** point/monastery precinct around **27.742222, 85.364167**.  
**Introduction:** Hilltop Gelug Buddhist monastery known for meditation and Buddhist-study programs.  
**Things to do:** day visit when open, meditation/course participation by arrangement, monastery landscape and views.  
**Permit / entry:** no tourism permit; **day-visitor availability must be checked with the monastery before visiting**. Courses/programs have separate registration.  
**How to visit:** taxi from Boudha or central Kathmandu toward Kopan/Budhanilkantha; final access follows the monastery road.  
**Source:** Kopan Monastery official visitor guidance; NTB.

### 8. Pharping heritage and monastery circuit
**Type:** pilgrimage/cultural circuit  
**Geometry:** settlement-area center around **27.566667, 85.250000**, with child POIs to be pinned separately.  
**Introduction:** Southern valley-rim pilgrimage landscape combining historic Pharping, Buddhist monasteries/caves and Hindu sacred sites.  
**Things to do:** monastery visits, pilgrimage, heritage walk, meditation-site visits where open, combine with Dakshinkali.  
**Permit / entry:** no area-wide tourism permit identified; individual monasteries/caves may set access or donation rules.  
**How to visit:** road from central Kathmandu through the southern valley toward Pharping; use Pharping town as the visitor hub and route onward to individual sites.  
**Sources:** Nepal Tourism Board; Dakshinkali Municipality.

### 9. Dakshinkali Temple
**Type:** Hindu Shakti temple  
**Geometry:** temple point/precinct around **27.583333, 85.250000**; refine pin before turn-by-turn navigation.  
**Introduction:** Important Kali pilgrimage site on Kathmandu Valley's southern rim, especially active on Tuesdays, Saturdays and festival periods.  
**Things to do:** pilgrimage, observe temple landscape and surrounding valley; visitors should be aware that ritual animal sacrifice occurs on important worship days.  
**Permit / entry:** no separate tourism permit identified; local religious access rules apply.  
**How to visit:** road from Kathmandu via Pharping by bus/taxi/private vehicle; final approach on foot from the temple access area.  
**Source:** Nepal Tourism Board; Dakshinkali Municipality.

### 10. Chandragiri Hills
**Type:** hill station / cable-car destination / pilgrimage-recreation area  
**Geometry:** hill destination area around **27.666100, 85.205500**; bottom cable-car station must be a separate routing pin.  
**Introduction:** Western valley viewpoint complex with cable-car access, Bhaleshwor Mahadev Temple, mountain/valley panoramas and recreation facilities.  
**Things to do:** cable-car ride, viewpoints, temple visit, dining/recreation, short walks.  
**Permit / entry:** no travel permit; cable-car fares and operating hours are commercial/dynamic data and must be refreshed.  
**How to visit:** travel from central Kathmandu to Thankot/Chandragiri cable-car base, then use the cable car when operating. Road access and service interruptions should be checked live.  
**Source:** Chandragiri Hills official operator; municipality context.

### 11. Indra Daha and Dahachok
**Type:** hill/heritage landscape  
**Geometry:** area; exact visitor center/pin requires a dedicated coordinate pass.  
**Introduction:** Rural hill landscape west of Kathmandu associated with Indra Daha, Dahachok settlement, viewpoints and local heritage.  
**Things to do:** hill drive/hike, views, local religious/heritage exploration.  
**Permit / entry:** no district-level permit identified; local site rules dynamic.  
**How to visit:** road from Kathmandu/Thankot toward Dahachok; route planner should use a verified road-access node before publishing turn-by-turn directions.  
**Status:** publication-capable as an area; exact attraction-level pins need refinement.

### 12. Shivapuri-Nagarjun National Park — Kathmandu sector
**Type:** protected area  
**Geometry:** polygon/area, never one park pin.  
**Introduction:** Forested national park protecting Kathmandu Valley's northern and western watershed ridges, with hiking, biodiversity, religious sites and Bagmati headwaters.  
**Things to do:** hiking, birding, forest walks, Bagdwar route, Nagarjun/Jamacho route where open.  
**Permit / entry:** **national-park entry rules/fees apply**; current fees, gate hours and trail restrictions are dynamic. This is park entry, not a restricted-area trekking permit.  
**How to visit:** choose a real gate/trailhead such as Budhanilkantha/Shivapuri side, Sundarijal side or Nagarjun side according to the hike. Never route users to the park polygon center.  
**Source:** Department of National Parks and Wildlife Conservation / park authority.

### 13. Nagarjun–Jamacho hike
**Type:** hiking route  
**Geometry:** route/corridor inside the Nagarjun sector of Shivapuri-Nagarjun National Park.  
**Introduction:** Forest climb to Jamacho ridge/stupa area with Kathmandu Valley views.  
**Things to do:** day hike, forest/bird observation, viewpoint and religious stop.  
**Permit / entry:** national-park entry requirements apply; trail opening/security conditions must be checked.  
**How to visit:** from central Kathmandu travel to the current Nagarjun park entrance, complete entry formalities, then hike the authorized route.  
**Routing note:** start-gate coordinate should be refreshed from the park before navigation publication.

### 14. Sundarijal
**Type:** settlement / water-and-trail gateway  
**Geometry:** visitor area rather than waterfall-only pin.  
**Introduction:** Eastern Kathmandu Valley gateway where water infrastructure, forest scenery and trails lead into Shivapuri-Nagarjun National Park.  
**Things to do:** short walks, forest entry, start longer hikes, water-landscape viewing.  
**Permit / entry:** town access requires no permit; entering the national park requires current park entry formalities.  
**How to visit:** road/bus/taxi from central Kathmandu to Sundarijal; continue on foot through the appropriate park gate for park trails.  
**Routing:** use settlement/gate nodes rather than a generic river coordinate.

### 15. Bagdwar
**Type:** sacred spring / Bagmati source-area hiking destination  
**Geometry:** point/upper-ridge destination within the national park; exact trail pin should be maintained from park mapping.  
**Introduction:** High forest source area traditionally associated with the origin of the Bagmati River.  
**Things to do:** pilgrimage-oriented hike, forest trekking, watershed interpretation.  
**Permit / entry:** national-park entry rules apply.  
**How to visit:** normally approached on foot from an authorized Shivapuri-side trailhead; this is not a roadside POI. Visitors need current trail/weather information.  
**Safety:** steep forest trail; weather and daylight planning matter.

### 16. Taudaha Lake
**Type:** lake/wetland  
**Geometry:** water polygon with visitor-access edge pin, not center-of-water navigation.  
**Introduction:** Small lake on the Kirtipur-Dakshinkali corridor valued for birdlife, local recreation and religious folklore.  
**Things to do:** birdwatching, lakeside walking, photography, combine with Kirtipur/Pharping.  
**Permit / entry:** no special travel permit identified; current local access/parking rules dynamic.  
**How to visit:** road south/southwest from Kathmandu or Kirtipur toward Chobhar/Dakshinkali corridor; route to the public lakeside access, not the lake center.

### 17. Gokarna forest and temple area
**Type:** forest-religious landscape  
**Geometry:** area with separate temple/forest-access child pins.  
**Introduction:** Eastern valley landscape combining the Gokarneshwar temple tradition, Bagmati river corridor and forested surroundings.  
**Things to do:** temple visit, riverside/heritage exploration, forest-edge scenery.  
**Permit / entry:** no area-wide tourism permit; access inside controlled forest/property areas may differ.  
**How to visit:** taxi/bus from central Kathmandu or Boudha toward Gokarna; use the temple/settlement as the public navigation anchor.  
**Routing note:** do not imply unrestricted access to every forest parcel.

### 18. Narayanhiti Palace Museum
**Type:** museum / former royal palace  
**Geometry:** exact museum/entrance point in central Kathmandu.  
**Introduction:** Former royal palace converted into a museum interpreting Nepal's modern monarchy and palace spaces.  
**Things to do:** museum visit, modern political/royal history.  
**Permit / entry:** no travel permit; museum ticket, opening days, security and photography rules are dynamic.  
**How to visit:** easily reached on foot/taxi from Thamel and central Kathmandu; route to the public museum entrance rather than the palace grounds center.

### 19. National Museum of Nepal, Chhauni
**Type:** national museum  
**Geometry:** exact museum-complex point/area in Chhauni.  
**Introduction:** Nepal's principal national museum complex, with collections covering art, sculpture, history, arms and cultural heritage.  
**Things to do:** museum galleries, Nepali art/history study; combine with Swayambhunath.  
**Permit / entry:** no travel permit; museum fee/hours are dynamic.  
**How to visit:** short taxi from central Kathmandu/Thamel or combine with Swayambhunath; route to the museum entrance at Chhauni.  
**Source:** Nepal Tourism Board museum guidance.

## Permit and entry model
Kathmandu District has **no district-wide restricted-area trekking permit**. Visitor controls are destination-specific: UNESCO/heritage entrance fees, national-park entry, museum tickets, monastery visitor policies, religious-access restrictions and commercial cable-car tickets. Store these independently and timestamp all prices/rules.

## Dynamic fields
Never freeze these into permanent content without a fresh check:
- heritage entrance fees;
- Pashupati visitor/ritual access rules and puja pricing;
- national-park fees, gate hours and trail closures;
- Chandragiri cable-car fare/hours/maintenance closure;
- museum opening days/tickets;
- Kopan day-visitor availability;
- festival crowd controls;
- road/traffic travel times.

## QA / routing holds
- Indra Daha/Dahachok needs attraction-level pin refinement before turn-by-turn routing.
- Bagdwar should use a verified trail endpoint from park mapping, not a generic web pin.
- Gokarna forest must not imply public access across all forest/property areas.
- Taudaha navigation must terminate at a public shore/access node, never in the lake.
- National-park records must always resolve to a gate/trailhead before routing.

## Source hierarchy
1. UNESCO World Heritage Centre for component identity/boundaries.
2. Nepal Tourism Board and Government of Nepal tourism sources.
3. Pashupati Area Development Trust, DNPWC/park authority and municipality sources.
4. Individual official operators/institutions such as Kopan Monastery and Chandragiri Hills for operational data.
5. Secondary mapping only for coordinate refinement, clearly labeled and never used to invent access.
