## 1. Nuwakot Durbar complex

- **District:** Nuwakot
- **Municipality / area:** Bidur Municipality / historic Nuwakot hill settlement
- **Destination type:** Royal palace-fortress heritage cluster / historic old-town anchor
- **Core identity:** Established historic palace and fortress landscape strongly associated with the Shah-era unification of Nepal.
- **National tourism grounding:** Nepal Tourism Board lists **Nuwakot Durbar** among Bagmati Province's cultural, historical, archaeological and natural tourism products.
- **Cluster modeling:** Treat Nuwakot Durbar complex as the parent heritage cluster. Saat Tale Durbar, Bhairabi Temple, Taleju Temple and the old town remain linked child records rather than duplicate independent versions of the same destination.
- **Restoration/access caution:** Earthquake damage and restoration history mean individual structures must have their present public-access status rechecked before CMS publication. Do not infer that every palace interior is currently open.
- **Coordinates:** Use a verified heritage-cluster centroid/polygon only after GIS QA; individual monuments need separate pins.
- **Verification status:** **VERIFIED major heritage destination; structure-level access and GIS require current verification.**

## 2. Saat Tale Durbar

- **Data-modeling rule:** Do not merge this record into the generic Nuwakot Durbar complex; retaining a monument-level object allows future restoration/opening information to be updated independently.
- **Coordinates:** Exact building footprint/pin requires authoritative heritage/GIS verification.

## 3. Bhairabi Temple, Nuwakot

- **District:** Nuwakot
- **Destination type:** Hindu temple / historic royal-town shrine / pilgrimage site
- **Strong national tourism evidence:** Nepal Tourism Board identifies **Bhairabi, Nuwakot District** as a temple situated in a palace complex constructed in the Malla period and renovated in the Shah period, and notes a major fair in the Nepali month of Chaitra.
- **Cultural relationship:** Principal religious component of the Nuwakot heritage cluster and associated in the canonical inventory with the Sindure Jatra tradition.
- **Festival rule:** Do not hard-code Gregorian festival dates. Annual dates and procession/access arrangements must be verified from the relevant Nepali calendar and local authority/temple notices.
- **Religious etiquette:** Visitor copy should include respectful temple behavior and note that access/photography restrictions may apply; current rules require local verification.
- **Coordinates:** Temple-level pin requires GIS QA before publication.
- **Verification status:** **VERIFIED major pilgrimage/heritage shrine; annual festival operations and exact visitor rules are dynamic.**

## 5. Nuwakot old town

- **District:** Nuwakot
- **Municipality:** Bidur Municipality
- **Destination type:** Historic settlement / Newar cultural landscape / heritage walking area
- **Relationship:** Settlement fabric surrounding the Durbar, Bhairabi and other monuments; this is an area object, not a single POI.
- **Tourism value:** Traditional townscape, palace-temple relationships, historic streets and cultural landscape provide the context that makes the individual monuments intelligible as one destination.
- **Route-planner modeling:** Store an old-town polygon/heritage-walk area plus verified monument nodes. Do not place a generic pin on a private residence.
- **Community sensitivity:** Photography of residents, rituals and private property should be consent-based. Tourism copy should distinguish public heritage spaces from lived residential areas.
- **Restoration/access:** Streets and public areas may remain accessible even when individual monuments are under restoration; structure-level status must therefore be tracked separately.
