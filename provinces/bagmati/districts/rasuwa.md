## Coverage notes

- The former combined entry **“Chilime and Bhote Koshi valleys”** has been separated into clearer landscape and river-corridor records.
- Kyanjin Gompa village and the monastery are listed separately because one is a visitor settlement and the other is a religious site.
- Gosainkunda Lake is retained as the principal destination, while Saraswati Kunda, Bhairav Kunda and Surya Kunda are listed separately as identifiable lakes in the wider sacred wetland complex.
- Wildlife species are not treated as standalone places; they belong under Langtang National Park and its habitat zones.
- Technical climbing objectives are excluded unless they function as established trekking-peak products with clear permit requirements.
- Uttargaya/Betrawati is kept with a boundary note because the pilgrimage landscape connects Rasuwa and Nuwakot.
- Tatopani and Naukunda require stronger current local verification before full public guide pages are created.
