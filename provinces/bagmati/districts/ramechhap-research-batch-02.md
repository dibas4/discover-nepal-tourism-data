## 6. Sunapati Hill

- **District:** Ramechhap
- **Municipality:** Sunapati Rural Municipality
- **Destination type:** Religious-cultural hill / viewpoint / local pilgrimage landscape
- **Municipality identity:** Sunapati Rural Municipality states that the municipality itself is named after Sunapati Danda (hill), confirming the hill as a defining geographic and cultural feature of the local area.
- **Religious-cultural significance:** The municipality describes Sunapati Danda as a meeting point of former Hiledevi, Bethan and Khaniyapani local areas and as an important center for both Hindu and Buddhist religious/cultural practice.
- **Verified heritage elements:** The municipality homepage identifies a Mahadev temple, historic Buddhist mani/chorten features and a line of 108 mani structures on Sunapati Hill; it also states that the 108 mani are listed with Nepal's Department of Archaeology.
- **Current tourism identity:** Sunapati Municipality explicitly describes the hill as a tourism destination and uses tourism in its municipal development slogan.
- **Access context:** Municipality states that road access now connects all parts of the rural municipality, though only part of the road network is all-season; current road condition must still be checked before routing travelers.
- **Route-planner modeling:** Store as a hill/cultural-area object with separate Mahadev temple, chorten/mani, viewpoint and road/trail access POIs when coordinates are verified.
- **Coordinates:** Municipality gives a broad local-level reference near 27.47 N, 85.90 E; do not use this municipality-level coordinate as the exact hilltop attraction pin.
- **Primary sources:**
  - https://www.sunapatimun.gov.np/
  - https://sunapatimun.gov.np/
- **Verification status:** PASS — hill identity, religious-cultural importance and tourism role strongly verified from Sunapati Rural Municipality; exact hilltop/POI GIS remains pending.

## 10. Doramba memorial area

- **District:** Ramechhap
- **Municipality:** Doramba Sailung Rural Municipality
- **Destination type:** Conflict-memory / memorial landscape / sensitive historical site
- **Historical basis:** Doramba is the location of a documented conflict-era incident on 17 August 2003. Human-rights documentation records the detention and killing of Maoist cadres and civilians in Doramba during Nepal's armed conflict.
- **Memorial context:** Reporting on annual remembrance events identifies a memorial at Dandakatri/Dandakateri associated with those killed in the Doramba incident.
- **Sensitivity rule:** This must be written as a conflict-memory site, not entertainment or sensational `war tourism`. Traveler-facing copy should use neutral, source-based language and respect victims, families and contested historical interpretation.
- **Object-modeling rule:** Keep `Doramba memorial area` separate from the broader cultural village. The exact memorial, incident site and remembrance-event areas should become individual POIs only after authoritative local coordinates are verified.
