## 16. Ratanchura junar orchards

- **District:** Sindhuli
- **Area:** Ratanchura / Sindhuli junar-growing highlands
- **Destination type:** Citrus orchard landscape / agro-tourism area
- **Agricultural significance:** Sindhuli's junar (sweet orange) is a major district agricultural identity. Historical horticulture research records Ratanchura among the VDCs targeted by the One Village One Product program for junar production and marketing.
- **Current official ecosystem:** The Government of Nepal's PMAMP Sindhuli unit operates a dedicated Junar Digital Information System, confirming that junar remains an organized commercial crop in the district.
- **Seasonality:** Historical horticulture research places harvest broadly from November to February. Exact orchard-visiting and fruit-picking periods must be confirmed each season with farmers/local authorities.
- **Route-planner modeling:** Store Ratanchura as an orchard/agro-tourism area. Individual farms, collection centers, processing sites and viewpoints require opt-in/public-access verification before being exposed as visitor POIs.
- **Coordinates:** Orchard-belt polygon and individual visitor farms pending GIS/local verification.
- **Primary sources:**
  - https://sindhulijunar.pmamp.gov.np/
  - https://sindhuli.akc.gov.np/
  - Nepalese Horticulture vol. 8/9 study on Sindhuli junar production.
- **Verification status:** PASS for junar-production identity and Ratanchura's historic production role; farm-level tourism operations remain local/live checks.

## 20. Chure forest corridor

- **District:** Southern Sindhuli
- **Destination type:** Forest/ecological corridor / Chure landscape
- **Ecological identity:** Southern Sindhuli forms part of Nepal's Chure/Siwalik landscape, a region important for forests, watersheds and ecological connectivity.
- **Tourism modeling:** This is an ecological landscape/corridor object, not a park with a single gate. Individual community forests, trails, picnic areas and wildlife-viewing sites require responsible authority/community verification.
- **Conservation caution:** Do not turn wildlife-corridor data into precise public wildlife-location guidance. Traveler content should emphasize legal public routes and low-impact visitation.
- **Access:** Forest entry rules can vary by community forest and management authority; no district-wide access rule should be assumed.
- **Coordinates:** Use authoritative Chure/forest-cover geometry in the GIS layer.
- **Verification status:** PASS as a regional ecological landscape; visitor-ready sub-sites remain separate verification work.
