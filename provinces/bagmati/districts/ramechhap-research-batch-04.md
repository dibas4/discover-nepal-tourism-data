- **Area:** Eastern Ramechhap / Khimti corridor
- **Destination type:** River valley / agricultural and forest landscape / hydro corridor
- **Geographic evidence:** Government road maps identify Khimti and Khimti Khola in eastern Ramechhap and show the valley corridor in relation to Those, Bamti Bhandar and northern settlements.

## 17. Gumdel Village

- **District:** Ramechhap
- **Municipality:** Umakunda Rural Municipality
- **Destination type:** Northern mountain settlement / trekking-route village / cultural landscape
- **Geographic evidence:** Government road/district maps locate Gumdel in northern Ramechhap near the Likhu highland corridor and north of Chuchure.
- **Historic administrative evidence:** Nepal's 2011 census records Gumdel as a distinct former VDC with 497 households and 2,466 residents, confirming the settlement identity independently of tourism marketing.
- **Landscape evidence:** JICA data characterizes former Gumdel VDC as overwhelmingly upper-temperate terrain, consistent with its highland agricultural/mountain setting.
- **Tourism role:** Keep Gumdel as a settlement object linked to northern Ramechhap trekking/highland routes. Homestays, monasteries, cheese/yak production and specific cultural attractions must be verified individually before publication.
- **Coordinates:** Settlement polygon/center and trail/road access points require GIS QA.

## 18. Bamti Bhandar

- **District:** Ramechhap
- **Municipality:** Umakunda Rural Municipality

- **Geographic evidence:** Government district road maps locate Bamti Bhandar in northeastern Ramechhap on the mountain corridor between lower Ramechhap and the Jiri/northern highland approaches.
