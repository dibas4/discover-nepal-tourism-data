## 31. Manahari community tourism area

- **District:** Makwanpur
- **Local government:** Manahari Rural Municipality
- **Destination type:** Community/Indigenous tourism landscape; buffer-zone and river/forest cultural area
- **Administrative grounding:** Manahari Rural Municipality is an official local government in Makwanpur with nine wards and its administrative centre at Rajaiya, Ward 9.
- **Strong tourism evidence:** A Government of Nepal Ministry of Culture, Tourism and Civil Aviation homestay impact evaluation documents the **Bankariya Community Homestay** in Manahari Rural Municipality. The study places the Bankariya settlement in the Masine Shanti buffer-zone area associated with Parsa National Park and identifies Bankariya culture, local food, Chure foothill scenery, river valleys, forest landscape and possible wildlife sightings as tourism assets.
- **Historical operating-status caution:** The government study was based on a 2020 field survey and reported that the homestay was still preparing to open and was not yet legally registered at that time. Therefore this record must NOT claim that a registered/operating homestay is currently available without a live check.
- **Current-status caution:** Later reporting indicates the Bankariya homestay infrastructure has faced serious operational/disrepair problems. Treat lodging availability as dynamic and do not route travelers there as bookable accommodation unless reverified locally.
- **Community sensitivity:** Bankariya are a highly marginalized Indigenous community. Tourism copy must avoid treating residents as a spectacle. Any cultural visit should be framed around community consent, local benefit, respectful photography and locally managed experiences.
- **Route-planner modeling:** Keep `Manahari community tourism area` as an area/community-tourism object rather than a single POI. Potential child layers include verified community settlements, river/forest landscape, Parsa buffer-zone boundary, local access roads and any currently operating community-managed visitor facilities.
- **Coordinates:** Do not pin a generic community-tourism point to a house or settlement without authoritative/local verification. Municipality/buffer-zone polygons are preferable until precise visitor nodes are confirmed.
- **Wildlife/safety:** Buffer-zone wildlife encounters are not guaranteed and can involve safety risks. Never market wildlife sighting as assured. Current park/buffer-zone rules must be checked before publishing activities.
- **Season/access:** Monsoon road/river conditions can affect access; current local transport and road conditions are dynamic.
- **Verification status:** **VERIFIED AS A COMMUNITY-TOURISM LANDSCAPE; CURRENT HOMESTAY/ACTIVITY OPERATIONS REQUIRE LIVE LOCAL VERIFICATION.**
