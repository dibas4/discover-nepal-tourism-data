# Bhaktapur District — Visitor-Ready Tourism Enrichment

Complete visitor-ready enrichment for all 11 canonical records, with district introduction/history, area/point/route geometry, things to do, permits/entry, and how to visit from Kathmandu/Bhaktapur hubs. UNESCO monument-zone geometry is used for Bhaktapur Durbar Square and Changu Narayan; Nagarkot remains a shared Bhaktapur–Kavrepalanchok ridge object; Nil Barahi and Pilot Baba exact entrance pins remain final-GIS holds. Current heritage fees and commercial operations are dynamic and must be rechecked before traveler publication.
