## 16. Lapchi Valley

- **District:** Dolakha
- **Municipality:** Bigu Rural Municipality
- **Destination type:** Remote sacred Himalayan valley / trekking and pilgrimage region
- **Regional context:** Lapchi lies in northern Dolakha within the Bigu-side Himalayan landscape and is associated with Tibetan Buddhist pilgrimage traditions.
- **Access character:** This is a remote valley reached through long mountain approaches rather than a normal road-trip destination. Roadhead condition, bridge status, seasonal trail accessibility and border-area controls may change.
- **Religious-cultural significance:** The valley is strongly associated with Buddhist retreat traditions and Milarepa-related sacred narratives; traveler content should treat it as a living sacred landscape rather than a generic trek.
- **Route-planner modeling:** Store `Lapchi Valley` as a valley/route region with separate settlement, monastery, cave/meditation-site and trail objects. Do not collapse the valley into one POI.
- **Permit/access rule:** Because the valley is near the Tibet border, any restricted-area, immigration, conservation or local access rules must be verified from current official authorities before publication.
- **Coordinates:** Valley polygon and exact trail geometry require authoritative GIS/local mapping.

## 19. Tamakoshi River valley

- **District:** Dolakha
- **Regional span:** Central and northern Dolakha
- **Destination type:** Major river corridor / transport and settlement valley / hydropower landscape
- **Hydrological importance:** Tamakoshi is one of Dolakha's defining river systems and shapes access, settlements and infrastructure across central and northern parts of the district.
- **Tourism context:** For travelers, its value is primarily as a scenic and geographic corridor connecting mountain settlements and road routes rather than a single attraction.
- **Infrastructure context:** The valley also contains major hydropower infrastructure. Route-planner content should clearly distinguish public scenic/road access from industrial or restricted hydropower facilities.
