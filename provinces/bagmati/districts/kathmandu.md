### 1. Kathmandu Durbar Square / Hanuman Dhoka

- **District:** Kathmandu
- **Municipality:** Kathmandu Metropolitan City
- **Official / common names:** Kathmandu Durbar Square; Basantapur Durbar; Hanuman Dhoka Durbar Square
- **UNESCO component:** Kathmandu Valley, Hanuman Dhoka Durbar Square (121bis-001)
- **Coordinates:** 27.703889, 85.308333
- **Coordinate status:** UNESCO component coordinate
- **Address:** Digha Pahleza J P Setu Rd, Kathmandu 44600 (Kathmandu Metropolitan City listing)
- **Visitor fee (source checked 2026-08-08):** Foreign nationals NPR 1,000; BIMSTEC/SAARC listing NPR 500 on the current Nepal Tourism Board English fee table; Nepalese free; children below 10 free. Recheck before publication because fee tables can change.
- **Permit requirement:** No separate travel permit identified; site entrance fee applies.
- **Visitor note:** The square contains palace courtyards, temples and Kumari Ghar; some inner areas function as museums and individual access can differ.
- **Primary sources:**
  - https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/121/maps/
  - https://kathmandu.gov.np/archives/heritage/kathmandu-durbar-square?lang=en
  - https://ntb.gov.np/kathmandu-durbar-square
  - https://ntb.gov.np/plan-your-trip/before-you-come/heritage-site-entry-fees
- **Verification status:** Verified from UNESCO + government/NTB sources

### 2. Kirtipur heritage town

- **District:** Kathmandu
- **Municipality:** Kirtipur Municipality
- **Destination type:** Historic Newar hill town / living cultural landscape
- **Coordinates:** 27.666667, 85.283333
- **Coordinate status:** Municipality/town coordinate, not a single-attraction pin
- **Location context:** About 8 km southwest of central Kathmandu; Nepal Tourism Board describes Kirtipur as an ancient Newar town on a ridge.
- **Key heritage anchors confirmed by official municipality sources:** Bagh Bhairav Temple, Chilancho Stupa, Uma-Maheshwor Temple, Aadinath Lokeshwor and Jal Binayak.
- **UNESCO status:** Medieval Settlement of Kirtipur is on Nepal's Tentative List, not an inscribed World Heritage property.
- **Permit requirement:** No town-wide tourism permit identified in the official sources reviewed. Individual temples/attractions may have their own customs or access rules.
- **Entry fee:** No reliable municipality-wide entry fee found in the official sources reviewed; do not publish a fee without a dated local check.
- **Primary sources:**
  - https://ntb.gov.np/en/kirtipur
  - https://kirtipurmun.gov.np/about/heritage-sites?lang=ne
  - https://kirtipurmun.gov.np/heritage/baghbhairav/
  - https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q1517030
- **Verification status:** Core identity and municipality verified; town coordinate is approximate for the heritage settlement as a whole

### 5. Pashupatinath Temple / Pashupati area

- **District:** Kathmandu
- **Municipality / area:** Kathmandu Metropolitan City, Bagmati River / Gaushala-Pashupati area
- **UNESCO component:** Kathmandu Valley, Pashupati (121bis-006)
- **Coordinates:** 27.710523, 85.348840
- **Coordinate status:** UNESCO component coordinate
- **Visitor fee (source checked 2026-08-08):** Foreign nationals NPR 1,000 per day/per entry; current NTB table states Indian nationals are free; Nepalese free. Recheck before publication.
- **Permit requirement:** No separate travel permit identified; site entrance fee applies to tourists.
- **Religious access restriction:** Nepal Tourism Board states the main temple complex is open only to Hindus. Non-Hindus can observe from terraces across the Bagmati River to the east.
- **Photography / conduct:** NTB states photography is prohibited in the main temple complex and leather items including shoes, belts and cameras are forbidden within the temple complex.
- **Access context:** NTB places the temple about 5 km east of the city center; public transport/taxis can reach Gaushala followed by a short walk.
- **Primary sources:**
  - https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/121/maps/
  - https://ntb.gov.np/en/pashupatinath
  - https://ntb.gov.np/plan-your-trip/before-you-come/heritage-site-entry-fees
- **Verification status:** Verified from UNESCO + NTB sources
