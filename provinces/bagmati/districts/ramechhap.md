## Coverage notes

- Jata Pokhari and Panch Pokhari are retained as separate lake destinations.
- Thulo Sailung is a cross-district destination shared with Dolakha.
- Manthali and Ramechhap Airport are separate records because one is a town gateway and the other is transport infrastructure.
- Local sites without reliable mapped identities remain marked for verification rather than treated as fully established attractions.
