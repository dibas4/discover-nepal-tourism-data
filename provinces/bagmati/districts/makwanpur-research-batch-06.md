## 26. Chitwan–Parsa wildlife corridor
- **District context:** Southern/western Makwanpur and adjoining protected landscapes.
- **Destination type:** Ecological connectivity landscape / conservation corridor.
- **Evidence:** Parsa National Park officially extends into Makwanpur and connects westward with Chitwan National Park; Chitwan National Park also extends across Makwanpur according to UNESCO.
- **Modeling:** Do not create this as a conventional tourist POI or imply unrestricted wildlife viewing. Store as a conservation-landscape/corridor layer linked to the canonical Chitwan and Parsa protected-area records.
- **Safety/conservation:** Visitor access must follow park/buffer-zone rules and authorized roads/trails. Never route travelers through habitat simply because GIS shows forest continuity.
- **Sources:** https://www.parsanationalpark.gov.np/index.php/en/about-pnp-2 ; https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/284/
- **Status:** VERIFIED as a cross-protected-area ecological landscape; exact corridor polygon requires conservation GIS.
