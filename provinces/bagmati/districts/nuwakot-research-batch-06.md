## 27. Northern Tamang villages

- **District:** Nuwakot
- **Category:** Community tourism and notable villages
- **Canonical area:** Northern Nuwakot, especially Dupcheshwar and Kispang areas
- **Disposition:** **VERIFIED AS A BROADER TAMANG CULTURAL/SETTLEMENT LANDSCAPE; SETTLEMENT-LEVEL TOURISM PRODUCT REMAINS PARTIAL.**

- **Operating-status caution:** Do not claim that a specific homestay, guide, cultural program or festival is currently available without a live local check.
- **Coordinates:** No generic pin. Use municipality/settlement polygons and verified child nodes later.
