## 11. Gosainkunda Lake

- **District:** Rasuwa
- **Municipality / area:** Gosaikunda Rural Municipality; inside Langtang National Park
- **Destination type:** Sacred alpine lake / pilgrimage destination / Ramsar wetland
- **International wetland status:** The Convention on Wetlands lists **Gosaikunda and Associated Lakes** as Ramsar Site no. **1693**, designated **23 September 2007**, with an area of **1,030 ha**.
- **Elevation:** Ramsar material identifies Gosaikunda at about **4,380 m** above sea level.
- **Hydrological significance:** Ramsar describes the lake as a high-altitude freshwater wetland whose seasonal meltwater helps recharge the Trishuli River system.
- **Religious significance:** Gosaikunda is a major Hindu pilgrimage site, especially during the annual Janai Purnima period. Exact festival dates vary by the lunar calendar and should be published only from dated official/local notices.
- **Protected-area relationship:** The lake lies within Langtang National Park, so park-entry rules and conservation restrictions apply in addition to normal trekking logistics.
- **Modeling rule:** Keep `Gosainkunda Lake` as the principal visitor POI within the larger `Gosainkunda lake system` wetland/landscape object.
- **Coordinates:** Use an authoritative lake centroid from Ramsar/DNPWC/GIS during map QA; do not substitute a lodge or generic trekking pin.
- **Safety:** High altitude, cold, snow/ice, rapid weather change and seasonal trail hazards require acclimatization and current local conditions checks.
- **Verification status:** **VERIFIED major sacred alpine lake and Ramsar destination; live access/park operations require current check.**
