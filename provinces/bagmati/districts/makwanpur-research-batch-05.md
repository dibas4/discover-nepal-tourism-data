## 25. Parsa National Park sector
- **District:** Makwanpur sector of a cross-district protected area
- **Type:** National park / wildlife and conservation landscape
- **Official grounding:** Parsa National Park states that the park extends across Parsa, Bara and Makwanpur and is connected to Chitwan National Park to the west. It was originally established as Parsa Wildlife Reserve in 1984; later expansion brought the stated park area to 627.39 km².
- **Habitat:** Official park information describes predominantly tropical/subtropical forest, especially sal, with riverine forest and Siwalik vegetation; the park supports elephant, tiger, leopard, sloth bear, gaur and other wildlife.
- **Canonical modeling:** Maintain ONE Parsa National Park parent destination. Makwanpur should reference its district sector rather than duplicate the entire park as a separate destination.
