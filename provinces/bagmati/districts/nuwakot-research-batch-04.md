## 19. Kispang Village

- **District:** Nuwakot
- **Local government:** Kispang Rural Municipality
- **Destination type:** Community/rural cultural landscape
- **Administrative grounding:** Kispang Rural Municipality is officially based at Kahule and consists of five wards. Public municipal records confirm the local-government identity and settlement context.
- **Important naming caution:** `Kispang Village` should not be modeled as though Kispang were one compact tourist village. The municipality contains multiple settlements/wards; the canonical label is better treated as a community-tourism area until a specific visitor settlement is selected.
- **Emerging tourism evidence:** Bhalche within Kispang has recent secondary tourism coverage describing highland scenery, Tamang culture, pastoral/agricultural life and homestay potential. This supports tourism potential but does not justify transferring every Bhalche claim to all of Kispang.
- **Coordinates:** Use municipality/settlement polygons or verified individual villages, not a fabricated central tourism pin.
- **Verification status:** **VERIFIED AS A COMMUNITY LANDSCAPE; RENAME/CHILD-POI MAPPING RECOMMENDED BEFORE PUBLICATION.**
