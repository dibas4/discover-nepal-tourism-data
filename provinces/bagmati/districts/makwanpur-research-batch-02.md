## 6. Kulekhani Reservoir / Indra Sarovar
- **District:** Makwanpur
- **Municipality:** Indrasarowar Rural Municipality
- **Destination type:** Artificial reservoir / hydropower landscape / fisheries and recreation area
- **Official context:** Bagmati Province's Fisheries Development Centre states that Indra Sarovar lies in northern Makwanpur at about 1,500 m elevation and was created on the Kulekhani River for hydropower generation.
- **Scale:** Government source reports about 216 ha at full level, around 55 ha in winter, roughly 7 km length and up to about 500 m width.
- **Hydrology:** Palung Khola, Chitlang Khola, Seti Khola, Chakhel Inlet, Chalkhu Khola, Thado Khola and Dhasku Khola are identified as contributing sources.
- **Naming/modeling:** Preserve `Kulekhani Reservoir` and `Indra Sarovar` aliases; model reservoir polygon separately from Markhu, dam/viewpoint and boat-launch records.
- **Operations:** Boating operators, prices, safety rules and hours require live verification.
- **Sources:** https://fdckulekhani.bagamati.gov.np/officeDetail/introduction?language=en ; https://ntb.gov.np/
- **Status:** Strong government verification; visitor operations remain dynamic.
