- **Destination type:** Political-history landscape / ancestral and memorial area

## 12. Marin Valley

- **District:** Sindhuli
- **Municipality:** Marin Rural Municipality and adjoining Marin river catchment
- **Destination type:** Inner-valley rural landscape / agriculture-and-river corridor
- **Municipality context:** Marin Rural Municipality's official development identity emphasizes agriculture, livestock and infrastructure, supporting the valley's productive rural-landscape character.
- **Hydrological importance:** Nepal's Sunkoshi Marin Diversion Multipurpose Project officially identifies the Marin River as a Bagmati tributary and is designed to transfer Sunkoshi water into the Marin system.
- **Tourism modeling:** Treat Marin Valley as an area/landscape object with villages, farms, river segments, forests and viewpoints as separate sub-objects rather than one attraction pin.
- **Infrastructure caution:** The nationally important diversion project can affect access, construction traffic and landscape conditions in parts of the corridor. Traveler-facing routing should use current road/project information.
- **Coordinates:** Valley/catchment polygon and specific visitor nodes require GIS QA.
- **Primary sources:**
  - https://marinmun.gov.np/
  - https://www.smdmp.gov.np/pages/introduction-1/
  - https://dwri.gov.np/content/140/sunkoshi-s-marine-diversion-multi-purpose-project/
- **Verification status:** Municipality and Marin hydrological identity verified; tourism POIs within the valley remain later enrichment.

## 13. Kamala River valley

- **District:** Sindhuli
- **Area:** Kamalamai Municipality and downstream eastern/southern Sindhuli
- **Destination type:** River valley / cultural-pilgrimage and agricultural landscape
- **Official identity:** Kamalamai Municipality states that the municipality was named for goddess Kamalamai at the source of Nepal's Kamala River, confirming the river's strong relationship to local geographic and religious identity.
- **Destination relationship:** Kamalamai Temple should remain a separate religious POI linked to the broader Kamala river/valley landscape.
- **Route-planner modeling:** Store the river as line geometry and the valley as an area/corridor. Bridges, ghats, temples, wetlands and recreation points should be separate verified POIs.
- **Seasonality/safety:** Monsoon flow, flooding, erosion and road conditions can change rapidly; no static riverbank-access assumption should be published.
- **Coordinates:** Authoritative hydro/GIS line and valley geometry required.
- **Primary source:** https://kamalamaimun.gov.np/en/content/brief-introduction
- **Verification status:** River-source/local-name relationship verified from municipality; site-level visitor nodes pending.

## 14. Sunkoshi River corridor

- **District context:** Northern Sindhuli boundary/corridor
- **Municipality:** Sunkoshi Rural Municipality and neighboring northern local levels
- **Destination type:** Major river corridor / road-trip landscape / rafting and riverside cultural zone
- **Municipality tourism evidence:** Sunkoshi Rural Municipality explicitly identifies rafting on the Sunkoshi River as a tourism activity and lists temples, monasteries and other attractions in the municipality.
- **National infrastructure context:** The Sunkoshi Marin Diversion project confirms the Sunkoshi as the source river for the inter-basin diversion toward the Marin River in Sindhuli.
- **Route-planner modeling:** Store the Sunkoshi as river-line/corridor geometry. Raft put-ins/take-outs, bridges, beaches, settlements and cultural sites must be separate verified POIs.
- **Operational caution:** Rafting suitability, discharge, monsoon hazards and access are live conditions. Do not encode fixed rafting availability from static pages.
- **Coordinates:** Authoritative river geometry plus verified access nodes required.
- **Primary sources:**
  - https://sunkoshimunsindhuli.gov.np/
  - https://www.smdmp.gov.np/pages/introduction-1/
- **Verification status:** River tourism role and municipality relationship verified; operator/access-node layer pending.
