## Coverage notes

- The previous combined record **“Jharlang and Darkha villages”** has been separated because they are distinct settlements.
- Ruby Valley is retained as a trekking region, while Somdang, Tipling, Sertung, Chalish, Borang and Lapa are separate community destinations.
- Ganga Jamuna Waterfall and the pilgrimage area are separate tourism products: one natural, one religious.
- The Trishuli rafting corridor is distinct from Benighat and Malekhu, which function as gateways and visitor stops.
- Dabjong, Jyamrung and Maidi heritage records should not receive detailed public guide pages until exact structures, coordinates and access are locally verified.
- Technical Ganesh Himal climbing peaks are not listed as ordinary tourist attractions unless they form part of an established trekking product.

## Sources used for this expansion

- Nepal Tourism Board and trekking references for Ruby Valley, Ganesh Himal, Pangsang Pass and the Chitwan Chepang Hill Trail.
- Dhading district and local-level references for rivers, municipalities, temples and heritage areas.
- Tripurasundari, Ganga Jamuna, Ruby Valley, Khaniyabas, Netrawati Dabjong and Siddhalek local-level references.
- Established rafting references for the Trishuli corridor and highway gateways.
