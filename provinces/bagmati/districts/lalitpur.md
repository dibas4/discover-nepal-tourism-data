### 1. Patan Durbar Square

- **District:** Lalitpur
- **Municipality:** Lalitpur Metropolitan City
- **Destination type:** UNESCO heritage monument zone / former royal palace square / living Newar heritage core
- **Name context:** Patan and Lalitpur are both established names for the historic city; preserve both for search and destination aliases.
- **UNESCO status:** Patan Durbar Square is one of the seven monument zones that make up the Kathmandu Valley World Heritage property.
- **Municipality heritage context:** Lalitpur Metropolitan City identifies Patan Durbar area and surrounding monuments as the city's World Heritage zone and lists Sundari Chowk, Mul Chowk, Keshav Narayan Chowk and Krishna Mandir among its principal elements.
- **Tourism context:** Nepal Tourism Board describes Patan as the city of artisans and Patan Durbar Square as the former palace center of the kings of Patan, surrounded by palace buildings, courtyards and pagoda temples.
- **Craft context:** NTB highlights the city's continuing metalworking traditions, including repoussé and lost-wax sculpture techniques. This is useful for future `things_to_do` and cultural-experience data rather than treating the square only as architecture.
- **Permit requirement:** No separate travel permit identified; heritage-site entrance fee applies to applicable visitor categories.
- **Fee caution:** NTB confirms that entrance fees must be paid before touring Patan Durbar Square, but exact tariffs are time-sensitive and should be captured from the current official fee table immediately before traveler-facing publication.
- **Coordinates:** Use the UNESCO monument-zone coordinate / authoritative palace-square geometry during the GIS pass rather than a generic Lalitpur city centroid.

### 5. Kumbeshwar / Kumbheshwar Temple

- **District:** Lalitpur
- **Municipality / area:** Lalitpur Metropolitan City, Patan
- **Destination type:** Hindu Shiva temple / pilgrimage site / historic pagoda architecture
- **Alias normalization:** Preserve `Kumbeshwar` and `Kumbheshwar`; NTB currently uses Kumbheshwar in its pilgrimage material.
- **Historical context:** Nepal Tourism Board states that the temple dates to 1392 and identifies it as the oldest temple in Patan.
- **Architecture:** NTB describes it as a five-storey wooden pagoda, one of only two five-storeyed temples in the Kathmandu Valley.
- **Religious context:** Dedicated to Shiva; NTB notes a nearby pond traditionally believed to receive water from Gosainkunda in Rasuwa.
- **Festival context:** NTB identifies Janai Purnima in August as the period of a major fair at the site. Publish the exact Gregorian festival date only from the relevant year's calendar/local notice.
