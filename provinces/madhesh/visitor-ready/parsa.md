# Parsa — Visitor-ready enrichment
Canonical records: 21.

## District introduction
Parsa combines Birgunj's border-city culture and trade heritage with Parsa National Park, Thori's forest/Chure gateway, urban pilgrimage, wetlands and historic settlements.

## Main hubs and access
Primary hub: Birgunj. Secondary hub: Thori for the western forest-border landscape. Park visitors must be routed to a current authorized gate/visitor zone. Birgunj–Raxaul is an international border node; crossing procedures, opening conditions and documents are dynamic.
