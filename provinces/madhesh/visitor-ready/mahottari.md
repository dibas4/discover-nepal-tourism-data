# Mahottari — Visitor-ready enrichment
Canonical records: 20.

## District introduction
Mahottari combines the water-centred Jaleshwar Mahadev pilgrimage landscape, Matihani's monastic and Parikrama heritage, northern Shiva pilgrimage and the Bardibas–Chure transition.

## Main hubs and access
Primary hubs: Jaleshwar for southern pilgrimage, Bardibas for northern/Chure access, Janakpurdham as the nearest major tourism base. Parikrama stops are a linked route, not isolated pins.

## Canonical visitor records
Jaleshwar Mahadev Temple; Jaleshwar sacred pond/water complex; Lakshminarayan Matha (Matihani Matha); Matihani Matkor pond; Parikrama Gachhi; Pancheshwar Nath; Mithila Madhya Parikrama – Mahottari sector; Marai Sthan; Dhruva Kunda; Dhruva Mandir; Kanchanvan; Tuteshwar Nath; Raimandal; Thadeshwarnath; Thadi Pokhari; Sonamai Sthan; Bardibas Chure foothills; Gauridanda/Gauribas; Ratu River corridor; Gaushala forest/wetland belt.

## Geometry and routing
Jaleshwar temple + water: linked precinct/polygon. Matihani Matha: precinct; Matkor pond: polygon. Parikrama: ROUTE with halt nodes. Chure: AREA. Ratu: CORRIDOR. Local ponds: polygon only after boundary verification.

## Permits and entry
No district-wide permit. Parikrama participation has religious/event logistics rather than a restricted trekking permit. Temple offerings/local tickets are separate. Chure road/trail access and flood conditions are dynamic.

## Things to do
Jaleshwar pilgrimage; Matihani monastery/learning heritage; Parikrama route interpretation; Dhruva Kunda/Kanchanvan pilgrimage; Tuteshwar day trip; Bardibas foothill nature excursions.

## HOLD/dynamic
Raimandal, Thadi Pokhari, Sonamai Sthan and generic Gaushala nature belt stay ROUTING_HOLD until exact access is verified. Parikrama dates, temporary camps and crowd conditions are dynamic.
