# Sarlahi — Visitor-ready enrichment
Canonical records: 17.

## District introduction
Sarlahi's visitor layer combines Bharat Tal recreation, Nunthar pilgrimage, Sagarnath and Chure forests, Murtiya heritage, wetlands, rivers and emerging agro-tourism.

## Main hubs and access
Primary hubs: Lalbandi, Bagmati Municipality, Ishwarpur and Malangwa. East–West Highway is the principal spine. Nunthar is a shared Bagmati destination and should not be duplicated from Rautahat.

## Canonical visitor records
Bharat Tal; Nunthar pilgrimage/riverside; Sagarnath Forest; Nadhi Tal; Murtiya archaeological temple ruins; Mukteshwar Temple; Mukteshwar Pokhari; Malhaniya Pokhari; Bihani Ban; Rajdevi Temple Malangwa; Lakhandehi River; Bagmati River; Chure foothill forest belt; Lalbandi northern forest routes; Ishwarpur forest/wetland belt; Bagmati lakeside recreation zone; Sarlahi sugarcane/rural agro-landscape.

## Permits and entry
No blanket permit. Boating/tickets at Bharat Tal are operational fees. Forest access may have local management rules. River access is seasonal; monsoon routing must be suppressed when unsafe.

## Things to do
Boating/lakeside recreation when operating; pilgrimage and riverside visit at Nunthar; forest/nature observation; Murtiya heritage exploration after access verification; rural/agricultural interpretation through verified hosts only.
