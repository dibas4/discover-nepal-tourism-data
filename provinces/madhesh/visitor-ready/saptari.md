# Saptari — Visitor-ready enrichment
Canonical records: 18.

## District introduction
Saptari combines Mithila pilgrimage, historic Rajbiraj, and the dynamic Koshi floodplain. The visitor model must separate stable temples/towns from seasonal wetlands, sandbars and protected wildlife landscapes.

## Main hubs and access
Primary hubs: Rajbiraj, Kanchanrup/Bhardaha and the East–West Highway. Route temple visitors from Rajbiraj or the highway; route Koshi visitors only to verified reserve/barrage observation or entry nodes. Never route to a floodplain centroid.

## Canonical visitor records
Chinnamasta Bhagwati Temple; Kankalini Temple, Bhardaha; Shambhunath Temple; Rajdevi Temple; Chandi Bhagwati Temple; Bhagwati Pokhari; Shree Raja Ji Than Temple; Manraja Gadhi heritage area; Rajbiraj planned old town; Koshi Tappu Wildlife Reserve – Saptari sector; Koshi Tappu wetland and birding landscape; Koshi Barrage eastern approaches; Saptakoshi River floodplain; Saptakoshi river islands and sandbars; Bhardaha wetland belt; Rupani wetland and forest belt; Kanchanrup/Bhar Daha wetland area; Hanumannagar–Koshi riverside cultural landscape.

## Geometry and routing
Temples: POINT/precinct after entrance verification. Rajbiraj: AREA. Bhagwati Pokhari and wetlands: POLYGON/AREA with shoreline access. Koshi Tappu: protected-area sector polygon linked to shared parent. Saptakoshi: CORRIDOR/FLOODPLAIN. Islands/sandbars: DYNAMIC_AREA and no fixed route.

## Permits and entry
No district-wide permit. Koshi Tappu reserve visits inherit current DNPWC entry and activity rules. Temple offerings/festival charges are not permits. Barrage/security and river access are dynamic.

## Things to do
Mithila/Shakti pilgrimage; Rajbiraj heritage walk; birdwatching and wetland interpretation; Koshi landscape viewing from authorized safe points; seasonal religious fairs.

## HOLD/dynamic
Manraja Gadhi exact public remains, Rupani/Bhardaha/Kanchanrup wetland access, river islands/sandbars and changing observation points remain ROUTING_HOLD or dynamic until locally verified.
