# Bara — Visitor-ready enrichment
Canonical records: 20.

## District introduction
Bara's visitor identity spans Gadhimai pilgrimage, the medieval Simraungadh archaeological landscape, Parsa National Park's Bara sector, and the Amlekhganj–Pathlaiya–Nijgadh Chure/forest gateway.

## Main hubs and access
Primary hubs: Kalaiya, Simara/Pathlaiya, Nijgadh and Birgunj for western approaches. Simraungadh and Bariyarpur are road excursions. Park visitors must use a currently authorized park gate rather than an arbitrary Bara forest coordinate.

No blanket permit. Parsa National Park sector inherits current park entry/activity rules. Gadhimai festival arrangements are event-specific. Archaeological access rules and local tickets must be checked separately.
