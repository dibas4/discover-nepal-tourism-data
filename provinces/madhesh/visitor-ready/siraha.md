# Siraha — Visitor-ready enrichment
Canonical records: 17.

## District introduction
Siraha's strongest tourism identity is the Salahesh cultural landscape around Lahan, complemented by river corridors, Chure foothills, local wetlands, pilgrimage sites and the Thadi border-market landscape.

## Main hubs and access
Primary hubs: Lahan, Siraha town and East–West Highway. Lahan is the main base for Salahesh Fulbari and northern excursions. Thadi is a controlled border gateway; crossing requirements stay dynamic.

## Canonical visitor records
Salahesh Fulbari; Salahesh Fulbari orchid site; Salahesh Temple/Sthan; Salahesh Palace/Gadhi area; Pakadi Gadhi remains; Uttarayani Dham; Dhamini Than; Patari Mela site; Aakash Ganga; Baba Taal; Kamal Daha; Haathi Daha; Kamala River corridor; Balan River corridor; Chure foothill forest belt; northern Siraha forest/picnic landscapes; Thadi historic border market.

## Geometry and routing
Fulbari: AREA with entrance pin. Seasonal orchid: sub-area/seasonal phenomenon, not a separate fake point. Gadhi remains and temples: POINT/AREA only after verification. Rivers: CORRIDOR. Chure: broad AREA with named access nodes required. Wetlands: POLYGON/shore access.

## Permits and entry
No blanket permit. Ordinary cultural/forest day trips require no restricted-area permit. Local tickets, fair charges and border formalities are dynamic and separate.

## Things to do
Salahesh folklore and New Year cultural visit; seasonal orchid viewing without disturbing the site; pilgrimage; wetland observation; Chure nature excursions; historic border-market interpretation.
