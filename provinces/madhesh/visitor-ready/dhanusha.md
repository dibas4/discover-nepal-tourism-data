# Dhanusha — Visitor-ready enrichment
Canonical records: 24.

## District introduction
Dhanusha is the core of Janakpurdham's Mithila and Ramayana pilgrimage landscape: monumental temples, sacred ponds, ritual routes, Mithila art and the Dhanushadham forest-pilgrimage system.

## History/identity
Janakpur is traditionally associated with ancient Mithila and King Janaka and is one of Nepal's most important Hindu pilgrimage cities. Visitor content must distinguish religious tradition from claims requiring archaeological proof.

## Main hubs and access
Primary hub: Janakpurdham. City-core sites should be walk/rickshaw/e-rickshaw clusters rather than separate car routes. Dhanushadham is a road excursion from Janakpur. Railway services and border operations are dynamic.

## Geometry and routing

## Permits and entry
No district-wide permit. Temple access is normally pilgrimage/public access subject to site rules. Forest entry/activity rules and any local tickets are separate. Festival dates, crowd controls, railway timetable and cross-border requirements are dynamic.

## Things to do
Janakpur sacred-core walk; pond/ghat circuit; Mithila art and craft visit; evening religious atmosphere at Ganga Sagar; Dhanushadham temple + forest excursion; Parikrama pilgrimage when seasonally active; railway heritage interpretation.
