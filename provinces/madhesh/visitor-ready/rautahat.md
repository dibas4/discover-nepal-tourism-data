# Rautahat — Visitor-ready enrichment
Canonical records: 16.

## District introduction
Rautahat combines Bagmati pilgrimage at Nunthar, Rajdevi devotion, Gaur's border-town culture, Maulapur/Patoura archaeology, Najarpur religious-community identity and northern Chure forests.

## Main hubs and access
Primary hubs: Chandrapur for Nunthar/forests, Gaur for southern heritage and border culture, Maulapur for archaeological/religious sites. Border crossing procedures remain dynamic.

## Canonical visitor records
Nunthar pilgrimage/riverside; Rajdevi Temple; Gaur historical town core; Maulapur archaeological/temple area; Patoura archaeological site; Najarpur vegetarian village; Shree Krishna Pranami Mandir Najarpur; Jungle Sahiya forest; Chure forest/hill belt; Bagmati River; Bagmati river beaches/picnic banks; Lal Bakaiya River; Chandrapur forest gateway; Maraghar wetland; Patharghat historical landscape; Rautahat border-market cultural corridor.

## Things to do
