## Province-level QA decisions

- Major pilgrimage landscapes are modeled as clusters/routes rather than collapsed generic places: Janakpur, Mithila Madhya Parikrama, Jaleshwar/Matihani, Gadhimai/Bariyarpur and Salahesh landscapes.
- Parsa National Park is a single cross-district protected-area destination with district-sector/gateway relationships rather than duplicated full park pages.
- Nunthar is one shared Bagmati River destination linked from Rautahat and Sarlahi.
- Koshi Tappu records in Saptari are separated into official protected-area sector, wetland landscape, barrage approaches and dynamic floodplain features.
- River corridors, Chure belts, forests, wetlands and sandbars are modeled as area/linear/dynamic geometry rather than arbitrary single pins.
- Archaeological claims remain PARTIAL/HOLD where surviving remains, boundaries or public access are not strongly verified (for example Patoura, Parsagadhi, Manraja Gadhi, Salahesh/Pakadi Gadhi).
- Small ponds/wetlands and emerging tourism sites remain PARTIAL/HOLD when current water extent, access or visitor readiness cannot be reliably established.
- Festival dates, border procedures, park fees, safari operations, boating, public access and seasonal river conditions remain dynamic fields.
