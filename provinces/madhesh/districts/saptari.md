## Coverage notes

- Chinnamasta Bhagwati, Kankalini, Shambhunath, Rajdevi and Chandi Bhagwati are retained as separate pilgrimage products.
- Bhagwati Pokhari is separated from Chandi Bhagwati Temple because the pond has its own religious and wetland use.
- Shree Raja Ji Than and Manraja Gadhi are separated: one is an active shrine, while the broader archaeological landscape requires verification.
- Koshi Tappu Wildlife Reserve, the wetland landscape, Koshi Barrage and the wider Saptakoshi floodplain are related but distinct visitor and conservation records.
- River islands, sandbars and smaller wetlands must not receive fixed-condition descriptions without recent seasonal verification.

## Source basis for this expansion

- Government-recognized cultural-monument references and Saptari district heritage research for major temples and historic sites.
- Department of National Parks and Wildlife Conservation information for Koshi Tappu Wildlife Reserve.
- Municipality and established destination references for Chinnamasta, Kankalini, Shambhunath, Rajdevi, Chandi Bhagwati and Manraja.
