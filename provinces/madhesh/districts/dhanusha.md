## Coverage notes

- Janaki Mandir, Vivah Mandap, Ram Mandir and Rajdevi Mandir remain separate records because each functions as a distinct visitor and pilgrimage site.
- Dhanushadham Temple and Dhanushadham Protected Forest are separate religious and ecological products.
- Sacred ponds are recorded individually rather than being merged into a generic “Janakpur ponds” entry.
- Festival landscapes and pilgrimage routes are included as tourism products, but they require seasonal dates and crowd-management information before publication.
- Newly developed features at Manimandap should be checked for completion, opening hours and current public access.

## Sources used for this expansion

- Janakpurdham and Dhanushadham local references for temples, pilgrimage routes and the protected forest.
- Cultural and heritage references for Rajdevi Mandir, Rajarshi Janak Mandir, Manimandap and Mithila art.
