## Coverage notes

- Nunthar is a cross-district Bagmati River destination and should be linked to both Rautahat and Sarlahi without duplicating it as two unrelated places.
- Maulapur and Patoura are retained separately: Maulapur is the wider religious/archaeological destination, while Patoura refers to a specific reported archaeological site.
- Najarpur village and its Pranami temple are separate tourism products: one community-based and one religious.
- Seasonal river beaches and Maraghar wetland are explicitly marked for ground verification because their condition can change substantially.

## Sources used for this expansion

- Department of Archaeology-derived monument references for Rautahat heritage sites.
- District and municipal tourism references for Nunthar, Maulapur, Patoura, Chandrapur and major river landscapes.
- Published documentation of Rajdevi Temple and Najarpur’s religious-community tourism identity.
