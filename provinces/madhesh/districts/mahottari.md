## Coverage notes

- Jaleshwar Mahadev Temple and its water complex are separate because one is the shrine and the other is the defining ritual landscape.
- Lakshminarayan Matha, Matkor pond and Parikrama Gachhi are recorded separately as monastery, sacred water site and event landscape.
- Dhruva Kunda and Dhruva Mandir remain separate but connected records.
- The Mithila Madhya Parikrama is included as a route product, while each major Mahottari halt is separately indexed.
- Raimandal, Thadi Pokhari, Sonamai Sthan and the Gaushala belt should not receive detailed public pages until locally verified.

## Sources used for this expansion

- Pilgrimage and heritage references for Jaleshwar Mahadev, Matihani Matha, Parikrama Gachhi and the Mithila Madhya Parikrama.
- Local and cultural references for Dhruva Kunda, Kanchanvan, Marai Sthan and Pancheshwar Nath.
- District and municipality references for Bardibas, the Chure foothills, Ratu River and local heritage clusters.
- Settlement and heritage references for Anaittha, Thadeshwarnath, Thadi Pokhari and Sonamai Sthan.
