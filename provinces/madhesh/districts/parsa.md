## Coverage notes

- Parsa National Park itself is separated from the safari/visitor zone because park geography and actual visitor operations are different records.
- Thori is divided into gateway, village heritage, river/forest landscape and locally promoted cave/spring sites.
- Ghadiarwa Pokhari and its Chhath festival landscape are separate: one is a physical wetland/urban attraction, the other is seasonal cultural tourism.
- Birgunj’s old market, railway/trade heritage, Ghanta Ghar and Shankaracharya Gate should not be collapsed into one generic city record.
- Sita Cave, Rai Dhara, Parsagadhi remains and buffer-zone community products require ground or authority verification before detailed publication.

## Sources and evidence used
