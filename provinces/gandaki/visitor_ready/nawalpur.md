# Nawalpur — Visitor-Ready Tourism Enrichment

## Introduction
Nawalpur is Gandaki's lowland wildlife and river district, combining the Narayani corridor, Chitwan National Park's western sector, community tourism, pilgrimage and Chure/highland landscapes.

## History
The district's identity is shaped by river communities, Tharu and Indigenous settlements, the Narayani–Gandak system and the newer administrative split from former Nawalparasi. Gaindakot and Kawasoti are the main modern gateways.

## Main hubs
Gaindakot for Narayani/Maula Kalika; Kawasoti for Amaltari and park-buffer travel; Binayi Triveni for Triveni/Gandak landscape.

## Permit logic
Park entry rules apply only when entering Chitwan National Park or regulated buffer-zone activities. General Gaindakot/Kawasoti/Triveni travel needs no park permit. Community safaris and boating must use currently authorized operators.

## Safety
Monsoon river changes, wildlife distance/ethics, barrage restrictions and community-forest opening status are dynamic.
