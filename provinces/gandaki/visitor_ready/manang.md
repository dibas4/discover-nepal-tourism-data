# Manang — Visitor-Ready Tourism Enrichment

## Introduction
Manang is a high-Himalayan trekking district on the Annapurna Circuit, with traditional villages, glacial lakes, monasteries, climbing peaks and the Thorong La crossing.

## History
The district's settlements developed along trans-Himalayan trade and pastoral routes. Tibetan Buddhist culture, stone villages and seasonal movement remain central to Manang's identity.

## Main hubs
Chame for district entry; Manang Village for acclimatization and services; Khangsar for Tilicho; Koto for Nar–Phu access; Yak Kharka/Thorong Phedi for Thorong La staging.

## Places
- **Annapurna Circuit, Manang section — ROUTE:** Chame–Pisang–Manang–Thorong corridor inside ACAP; current trekking/guide and conservation requirements apply.
- **Thorong La — PASS/ROUTE node:** very high seasonal crossing to Mustang; weather, snow and rescue conditions dynamic.
- **Tilicho Lake — WATER POLYGON/high-altitude objective:** route visitors to shoreline/trail approach, not lake centre.
- **Tilicho Base Camp route — ROUTE:** Khangsar-side trail with seasonal lodges and hazard checks.
- **Manang Village — AREA:** main acclimatization, cultural and lodging hub.
- **Braga Village and Monastery — AREA + POINT precinct:** traditional settlement and historic gompa.
- **Ngawal / Upper Pisang / Khangsar — AREA settlements:** heritage, mountain views and route staging.
- **Gangapurna Lake and Glacier — WATER/GLACIER AREA:** use safe public viewpoint/trail access; glacier edge changes.
- **Ice Lake (Kicho Tal) — WATER POLYGON + ROUTE:** strenuous acclimatization hike; route/snow conditions dynamic.
- **Milarepa Cave — POINT_HOLD:** religious cave; exact approach and identity require final local GIS confirmation.
- **Praken Gompa — POINT/precinct:** monastery/viewpoint above Manang; local opening etiquette dynamic.
- **Chame — AREA:** district headquarters and transport/trekking gateway.
- **Humde Airstrip — POINT/transport:** airfield; operational status must be live-checked before suggesting flights.
- **Yak Kharka / Thorong Phedi — AREA route settlements:** seasonal lodge clusters; not permanent urban destinations.
- **Chulu East/West landscape — MOUNTAIN AREA:** climbing objectives; climbing permits and technical route requirements separate from trekking entry.
- **Pisang Peak — MOUNTAIN objective:** climbing permit/guide requirements dynamic.
- **Nar–Phu access corridor — RESTRICTED ROUTE:** starts from Koto-side approach; restricted-area permit rules apply only to the covered Nar–Phu route/areas, not to all Manang travel.

## Permit logic
ACAP entry is separate from Nar–Phu restricted-area permission and from climbing permits. Thorong/Manang general travel must not inherit Nar–Phu restrictions by default.

## Safety
Altitude, snowfall, landslides, road closures, lodge seasons and medical/rescue access are dynamic and must be checked near travel date.
