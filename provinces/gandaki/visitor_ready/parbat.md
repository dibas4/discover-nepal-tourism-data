# Parbat — Visitor-Ready Tourism Enrichment

## Introduction
Parbat is a compact hill district centred on Kushma, known for deep river gorges, suspension bridges, commercial adventure activities, caves and rural highlands.

## History
Kushma developed as a district and trade hub above the Kali Gandaki and Modi valleys. Older villages and religious cave sites sit alongside newer bridge and adventure tourism.

## Main hubs
Kushma for bridges/adventure/caves; Jaljala/Salija for northern highlands; Phalebas for central-southern rural travel.

## Permit and access logic
No district-wide trekking permit. Commercial adventure activities use operator-specific rules, waivers and current safety status. Jaljala and Kali Gandaki are shared landscapes, not Parbat-only destinations.

## Safety
Bridge closures, commercial adventure status, cave access, monsoon river conditions and road conditions are dynamic.
