# Myagdi — Visitor-Ready Tourism Enrichment

## Introduction
Myagdi connects Beni and the Kali Gandaki corridor with hot springs, waterfalls, Gurung/Magar villages and remote Dhaulagiri trekking country.

## History
Beni grew at a strategic river confluence and trade junction. Northern Myagdi developed along the Kali Gandaki route, while western settlements provide access toward Dhaulagiri and Dhorpatan landscapes.

## Main hubs
Beni for district travel; Tatopani/Ghorepani side for Annapurna routes; Darbang for western Myagdi; Narchyang for waterfall and highland access.

## Permit logic
ACAP applies only in relevant conservation-area sectors. Dhaulagiri trekking and Gurja access are not automatically restricted-area travel, but expedition/climbing and local route requirements must be checked separately. Dhorpatan relationship is handled as a shared protected landscape, not duplicated here as a separate park.

## Routing holds
Exact Narchyang waterfall entrance and Takam Kot visitor point remain final GIS tasks. High-pass routes must never be routed from straight-line coordinates.
