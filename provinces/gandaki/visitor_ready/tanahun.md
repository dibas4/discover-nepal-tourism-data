# Tanahun — Visitor-Ready Tourism Enrichment

## Introduction
Tanahun combines Bandipur's preserved hill-town heritage with caves, river landscapes, literary history, pilgrimage and ridge viewpoints between Pokhara, Chitwan and Gorkha.

## History
The district preserves former principality landscapes, the birthplace tradition of poet Bhanubhakta Acharya, Newar trade-town heritage at Bandipur and sacred river/cave traditions around Damauli and Devghat.

## Main hubs
Bandipur for heritage/caves; Damauli/Byas for central district travel; Aanbukhaireni for eastern gateway connections; Devghat for pilgrimage.

## Permit/access logic
No district-wide tourism permit. Cave fees/guides, rafting, local temples and heritage facilities are operational products with dynamic rules. Devghat, Seti and Marsyangdi remain shared parent landscapes.

## Safety
Cave conditions, monsoon rivers, rafting availability, hill-road congestion and viewpoint weather should be checked before routing.
