# Gorkha — Visitor-Ready Tourism Enrichment

## Introduction
Gorkha combines Nepal's Shah-era political history with major pilgrimage, Gurung villages and the Manaslu–Tsum Himalayan system.

## History
Gorkha was the seat of the Shah rulers before Nepal's unification. The hill palace, Gorakhkali/Gorakhnath sacred complex and surrounding bazaar remain the district's strongest historic cluster, while northern Gorkha preserves Tibetan Buddhist valleys and long-distance trade routes.

## Hubs
Gorkha Bazaar for the historic core; Kurintar/Manakamana for cable-car pilgrimage; Arughat/Machha Khola/Jagat for Manaslu approaches; Philim for the Manaslu–Tsum junction.

## Places
- **Gorkha Durbar — AREA/precinct:** hill palace-fort-religious complex; history, architecture, panorama. No trekking permit; heritage access rules dynamic. Reach by road to Gorkha Bazaar then uphill approach.
- **Gorkha Museum — POINT:** Shah-era and district-history museum in bazaar; hours/fee dynamic.
- **Gorakhkali Temple — POINT/precinct:** major shrine inside Durbar landscape; religious conduct rules apply.
- **Gorakhnath Cave — POINT:** pilgrimage cave near Durbar; exact entrance and local access path should be used.
- **Gorkha Bazaar — AREA:** historic market and transport base.
- **Manakamana Temple — POINT/precinct:** national pilgrimage shrine. Reach by road to Kurintar and cable car or local road/walk alternatives.
- **Manakamana Cable Car — ROUTE + station POINTS:** commercial transport attraction; fare, hours and maintenance closures dynamic.
- **Manaslu Conservation Area — POLYGON:** protected mountain/cultural landscape. Conservation entry rules are separate from restricted-area permits.
- **Manaslu Circuit Trek — ROUTE:** major multi-day route through Chumnubri. Current restricted-area permit applies only to covered restricted wards; guide/agency rules dynamic.
- **Larkya La — PASS/ROUTE node:** high pass; snow, landslide and rescue conditions dynamic.
- **Manaslu Base Camp — POINT/AREA objective:** high-altitude side trip from Samagaun; weather/avalanche conditions dynamic.
- **Birendra Tal — WATER POLYGON:** glacial lake near Samagaun; use shoreline/trail access, never lake-centre routing.
- **Samagaun, Samdo, Lho, Chumling, Chhokangparo — AREA settlements:** trekking/cultural villages; lodging and monastery access dynamic.
- **Tsum Valley — AREA/CORRIDOR:** Tibetan Buddhist cultural valley with restricted-area rules where applicable.
- **Mu Gompa, Rachen Gompa — POINT/precinct:** remote monastery destinations; opening/visitor etiquette dynamic.
- **Barpak, Laprak — AREA villages:** Gurung heritage, earthquake history and village tourism; reach by current rural roads/trails from Gorkha-side hubs.
- **Budhi Gandaki Valley — CORRIDOR:** parent river/trail landscape for Manaslu routes.
- **Jagat, Machha Khola, Philim — AREA gateway settlements:** route staging nodes, not attractions collapsed into the whole circuit.

## Permit logic
Manaslu/Tsum restricted-area permits are separate from conservation entry. Current Immigration rules must be checked immediately before publication. Ordinary Gorkha Bazaar/Manakamana/Barpak visits do not inherit Manaslu permits.

## Routing holds
Remote monastery entrances, seasonal bridge status and high-pass safety must be refreshed before route-planner publication.
