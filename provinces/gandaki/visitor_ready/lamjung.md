# Lamjung — Visitor-Ready Tourism Enrichment

## Introduction
Lamjung is a Gurung cultural and trekking district built around Besisahar, the Marsyangdi Valley and the southern approach to the Annapurna Circuit.

## History
The district includes former hill principalities, historic settlements such as Gaunshahar and Kunchha, and long-established Gurung villages that became some of Nepal's best-known community-tourism destinations.

## Main hubs
Besisahar for Annapurna/Marsyangdi travel; Ghalegaun/Bhujung cluster for community tourism; Rainas and Madhya Nepal for eastern/southern heritage.

## Places
- **Besisahar — AREA:** district and trekking gateway; supplies, transport and onward route staging.
- **Annapurna Circuit, Lamjung section — ROUTE:** Besisahar–Marsyangdi corridor into ACAP; protected-area and current trekking rules apply only where relevant.
- **Marsyangdi River Valley — CORRIDOR:** major scenic/trekking valley; use road/trail access nodes, never river midpoint.
- **Ghalegaun — AREA:** flagship Gurung homestay/cultural village; homestay availability and local programs dynamic.
- **Bhujung — AREA:** large Gurung village with community culture and mountain views.
- **Pasgaun — AREA:** Gurung village and trekking/community destination.
- **Ghanpokhara — AREA/ridge village:** panorama and rural tourism.
- **Rainaskot — AREA/heritage ridge:** former fort landscape, village tourism and Himalayan view.
- **Lamjung Durbar — POINT/precinct:** former principality site at Gaunshahar; exact monument access dynamic.
- **Gaunshahar — AREA:** historic hill settlement linked to Lamjung Durbar.
- **Kunchha — AREA:** former district centre and heritage town.
- **Dordi River Valley / Midim River Valley — CORRIDORS:** rural scenery and community landscapes; specific visitor nodes need local mapping.
- **Lamjung Himal viewpoints / Himalchuli viewpoints — AREA_CLUSTER:** use named village/ridge viewpoints, not a generic mountain pin.
- **Nalma, Taksar — AREA_HOLD:** retain as village/heritage records until current homestay and public heritage assets are verified.
- **Udipur — AREA:** historic road/river gateway near Besisahar.

## Permit/access logic
Ordinary village and town travel needs no restricted-area permit. The Annapurna Circuit sector inherits ACAP/current trekking requirements only when the route enters regulated protected-area sections. Rural-road conditions and homestay operations remain dynamic.
