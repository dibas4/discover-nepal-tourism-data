# Mustang — Visitor-Ready Tourism Enrichment

## Introduction
Mustang is a trans-Himalayan district of Tibetan cultural landscapes, walled settlements, monasteries, pilgrimage sites, desert-like cliffs and the Kali Gandaki corridor.

## History
Lower Mustang grew around the Kali Gandaki trade route, Thakali settlements and Muktinath pilgrimage. Upper Mustang preserves the former Kingdom of Lo, centred on Lo Manthang, with palace, monastery and cave traditions.

## Main hubs
Jomsom for lower Mustang transport; Kagbeni for Upper Mustang gateway context; Muktinath/Ranipauwa for pilgrimage; Lo Manthang for Upper Mustang travel.

## Places
- **Lo Manthang — AREA/walled heritage town:** architecture, monasteries and cultural walks; restricted-area rules apply to covered Upper Mustang travel.
- **Lo Manthang Royal Palace — POINT/precinct:** historic palace; interior access/status dynamic.
- **Jampa and Thubchen monasteries — POINT/precincts:** major religious/art heritage; local hours/fees/photography rules dynamic.
- **Chhoser Cave Complex — AREA + cave entrance POINTS:** cliff caves; use verified public entrances only.
- **Niphu/Garphu monasteries — POINT cluster:** Chhoser-side monastery circuit.
- **Tsarang, Ghami, Dhakmar — AREA villages:** heritage settlements on Upper Mustang routes.
- **Ghar Gompa / Luri Gompa — POINT/precinct:** remote monastery destinations; route and opening conditions dynamic.
- **Damodar Kunda — WATER/AREA pilgrimage system:** remote sacred lakes; seasonal route, altitude and restricted-area rules apply.
- **Muktinath Temple — AREA/precinct:** major Hindu-Buddhist pilgrimage destination; ACAP context and local temple rules separated from Upper Mustang restricted permit.
- **Jwala Mai — POINT:** natural-flame shrine inside Muktinath landscape.
- **Kagbeni Old Village — AREA:** medieval settlement and gateway; Upper Mustang restricted boundary logic must be applied precisely rather than to all Kagbeni visits.
- **Kali Gandaki fossil corridor — CORRIDOR:** geological/pilgrimage landscape; fossil collection/transport rules must be checked before encouraging collection.
- **Jomsom — AREA:** airport/district/trekking hub; flight schedules highly weather-sensitive.
- **Marpha, Tukuche — AREA villages:** Thakali heritage, orchards, architecture and trade history.
- **Dhumba Lake — WATER POLYGON:** sacred lake; route to actual shore access.
- **Kali Gandaki Gorge — CORRIDOR:** cross-district river/gorge system.
- **Upper Mustang Trek — RESTRICTED ROUTE/AREA:** current Immigration permit required for covered wards; agency/guide rules dynamic.
- **Annapurna Circuit, Mustang section — ROUTE:** Muktinath–Jomsom side of shared route inside ACAP.
- **Thorong La approach, Mustang side — ROUTE:** Muktinath-side high-pass approach; snow/weather dynamic.
- **Tiji Festival landscape — EVENT + AREA:** Lo Manthang festival product; dates, crowd management and monastery rules annual/dynamic.

## Permit logic
Upper Mustang restricted-area permit, ACAP entry and climbing permits are separate products. Never apply the Upper Mustang permit automatically to all of Mustang or to ordinary Jomsom/Marpha/Muktinath visits.

## Safety
Flights, roads, snow, monsoon damage, monastery access and high-altitude route conditions remain dynamic.
