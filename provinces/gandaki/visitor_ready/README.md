## Visitor-ready schema
Every district file includes a district introduction and history, practical visitor hubs, short destination introductions, things to do tied to actual places, POINT/AREA/POLYGON/CORRIDOR/ROUTE geometry, permit and entry logic, access guidance, dynamic operational fields, and routing holds for unverified entrances or unsafe fake pins.

## Province-wide geography rules
- Annapurna Conservation Area is one shared protected landscape spanning Kaski, Lamjung, Manang, Mustang and Myagdi; district records describe sectors/routes rather than duplicate conservation areas.
- Manaslu Conservation Area and Tsum/Manaslu routes are modeled separately from Gorkha's normal heritage and pilgrimage destinations.
- Dhorpatan Hunting Reserve is one shared protected landscape across Baglung, Myagdi and adjoining Rukum.
- Chitwan National Park is one shared protected area; Nawalpur records represent Gandaki-side access/relationships, not a second park.
- Kali Gandaki, Marsyangdi, Seti, Narayani, Panchase, Jaljala, Thorong La and other cross-district systems remain shared parent routes/landscapes.

## Permit model
Protected-area entry, restricted-area permits and climbing permits are separate layers. ACAP entry does not imply an Upper Mustang, Nar–Phu or Manaslu restricted permit. Restricted permits apply only to the current covered routes/wards. Commercial cable cars, caves, boating, rafting, paragliding and adventure facilities keep prices/hours/safety status dynamic.

## Publication safety
No large settlement, lake, park, river or trekking landscape should be routed to a fake centre pin. Use area/polygon/corridor geometry and verified visitor-access nodes. Seasonal roads, flights, high-pass weather, monsoon rivers, lodge operation and commercial services must be refreshed before traveler-facing publication.
