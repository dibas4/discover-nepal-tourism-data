# Kaski — Visitor-Ready Tourism Enrichment

## Introduction
Kaski is Gandaki's main tourism hub, centred on Pokhara and extending north into Annapurna trekking country. The district combines lakes, caves, museums, religious sites, viewpoints, Tibetan heritage and major trekking gateways.

## History
Pokhara grew as a trade and administrative centre between the Himalayan foothills and western Nepal. Gurung villages, Tibetan refugee settlements, old bazaars and the Annapurna trekking network shape the district's modern tourism identity.

## Main hubs
Pokhara Lakeside for city tourism; Pokhara Old Bazaar for heritage; Hemja/Phedi/Kande for northern and ridge approaches; Ghandruk/Landruk/Dhampus for trekking routes.

## Permit and access logic
Pokhara city attractions require no trekking permit. ACAP entry applies only when entering the conservation-area trekking sectors. Commercial boating, paragliding, caves and museums use current operator/municipal rules rather than permanent hard-coded prices.
