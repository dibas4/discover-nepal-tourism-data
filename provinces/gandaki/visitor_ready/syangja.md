# Syangja — Visitor-Ready Tourism Enrichment

## Introduction
Syangja is a hill district of community tourism, river valleys, pilgrimage sites and ridge landscapes between Pokhara and the Kali Gandaki corridor.

## History
The district preserves former hill principalities, Gurung and Magar settlement traditions and long-used river and ridge routes. Sirubari became one of Nepal's best-known village-homestay destinations.

## Main hubs
Waling for central/southern travel; Putalibazar for district services; Arjun Chaupari for Sirubari; Ramdi/Chapakot for Kali Gandaki-side travel.

## Permit/access logic
No district-wide tourism permit. Panchase access inherits any current forest/community rules at the actual trailhead. Homestays and cultural visits should use active community operators rather than assumed availability.

## Routing rules
