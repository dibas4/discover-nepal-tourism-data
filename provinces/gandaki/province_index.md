## Verification model

- **Government-managed / protected:** formally protected areas, museums and public heritage sites supported by authoritative records.
- **Established:** widely recognized destination; coordinates, wards, fees or current access may still be pending.
- **First-round researched:** relevant inventory record requiring stronger municipality-level or specialist confirmation.
- **Needs local verification:** changing natural feature, private facility, community program, small structure or uncertain mapped location.
