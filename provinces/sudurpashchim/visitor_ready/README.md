## Visitor-ready standard
District enrichment follows the established Discover Nepal Hub model: district introduction/history, practical gateways, visitor meaning for canonical places, things-to-do relationships, POINT/AREA/CORRIDOR/WATER/ROUTE map behavior, access from realistic hubs, permit/entry handling, and ROUTING_HOLD where exact public access is not reliably verified.

## Province-wide parent systems
1. Khaptad National Park — one cross-district protected landscape with district-side access relationships in Doti, Achham, Bajhang and Bajura where applicable; route to verified gates/trailheads rather than park centroids.
2. Shuklaphanta National Park — Kanchanpur protected-area parent; safari/wildlife activity, gates, fees and closures remain dated operational data.
3. Api Nampa Conservation Area — one Darchula protected-area parent covering the high mountain conservation landscape; conservation-area entry is separate from restricted-area trekking rules.
4. Darchula restricted area — current Immigration system applies to Vyas Rural Municipality ward 1, not all of Darchula.
5. Bajhang restricted area — current Immigration system applies to Saipal Rural Municipality wards 1–5, not all of Bajhang.
6. Badimalika pilgrimage landscape — Bajura-centered sacred mountain/pilgrimage system with cross-district approach relationships; seasonal camps, weather and route state must be current.
7. Ramaroshan — Achham lake/wetland/highland complex; component lakes and trails require verified geometry instead of a single generic pin.
8. Ghodaghodi Lake Area — Kailali Ramsar wetland complex; model as a wetland system with component water bodies, habitat and sacred/community relationships.
9. Mahakali River corridor — international-border river system shared by Darchula, Baitadi, Dadeldhura and Kanchanpur relationships; crossings and border operations are dynamic.
10. Pancheshwar / river-confluence landscape — cross-border river and pilgrimage geography; never infer international access from map proximity.
11. Dodhara Chandani / Banbasa / Brahmadev border landscapes — route to verified Nepal-side public access; border/customs/security operations require current checks.
12. Tharu and Rana Tharu living-culture landscapes — community-led cultural records; private homes, ceremonies and sensitive locations require consent.

## Permit and entry model
- Protected-area entry and restricted-area trekking permits are separate legal layers.
- Current Department of Immigration table lists Bajhang Saipal RM wards 1–5 at USD 90 for the first week and USD 15/day beyond one week.
- Current Department of Immigration table lists Darchula Vyas RM ward 1 at USD 90 for the first week and USD 15/day beyond one week.
- Restricted-area permits must be handled through the current legal process and should never be generalized to an entire district.
- Park/conservation fees must be stored as dated operational data rather than permanent descriptive content.

## Dynamic-data policy
Never hard-code as timeless: border openings, customs/checkpoint operations, restricted-area fees, park/conservation fees, safari operations, flight schedules, road/bridge condition, monsoon closures, lake boating, rafting operators, pilgrimage dates/camps, trail condition, snow/high-pass safety, accommodation, ferries or community-tourism operations.

## Routing safety rules
- Lakes/wetlands route to verified public shoreline/access points, never water centroids.
- Parks/conservation areas route to official gates/checkpoints/trailheads, never protected-area centroids.
- Mountain, pilgrimage and long-distance trekking systems use route/corridor geometry.
- Border rivers and international crossings require verified Nepal-side access and current legal status.
- Heritage ruins and archaeological structures remain AREA/HOLD until authoritative public-access coordinates are verified.
- Wildlife records describe habitat, never guaranteed sightings.
- Living-culture, farm, pasture and sacred-community records require consent before exposing sensitive/private coordinates.

## Source backbone
Department of Immigration for restricted-area permits; Department of National Parks and Wildlife Conservation and Nepal Tourism Board for protected areas and current entry frameworks; Department of Archaeology/local governments for monuments; Ramsar/government sources for major wetlands; municipalities, park offices and communities for exact visitor access and facilities.
