## District quality notes

- Amargadhi Fort and Ajaymerukot require official heritage-condition and boundary verification before monument-level coordinates are published.
- Parashuram and Mahakali records are river and pilgrimage landscapes, not single-point attractions.
- Aalital and Ghatal require date-stamped checks for water, access, facilities, and safety.
- Community villages, homestays, small shrines, and seasonal waterfalls remain provisional until local verification.
