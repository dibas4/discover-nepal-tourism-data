## District cautions

- Badimalika and Budhinanda pilgrimage access, camps, weather, roadheads and festival facilities require date-stamped local verification.
- High-altitude sacred landscapes should not be reduced to single GPS points.
- Sensitive herb, pasture and community-culture records require local consent before detailed publication.
