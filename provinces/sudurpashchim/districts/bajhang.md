## Verification notes

- Saipal, Khaptad, rivers, valleys and pilgrimage routes are geographic entities and must not be reduced to misleading single coordinates.
- Surma Sarovar, Saipal trekking, remote roads, bridges, camps and accommodation require date-stamped ground verification.
- Former royal sites, temples and monument boundaries require Department of Archaeology, municipality or specialist confirmation before exact coordinates are published.
- Sacred Masta and Kedar traditions require respectful local consultation before detailed interpretation.
- Border and trans-Himalayan route information must use neutral language and current official security and permit rules.
