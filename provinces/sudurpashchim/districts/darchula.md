## Verification priorities

- Confirm all protected-area boundaries, fees, permits, checkpoints and trekking rules through current government records.
- Border routes, bridges, customs rules and Nepal–India or Nepal–China access must be date-stamped.
- Do not publish disputed-boundary claims as settled fact; use neutral wording and official Nepal government references.
- Preserve Sauka, Byansi and village-name aliases, but confirm preferred spellings locally.
- Mountain, valley, river and route records are geographic entities rather than single coordinates.
- Small temples, viewpoint nodes, camps, homestays, bridges and trail facilities require field verification.

- Nepal tourism and mountaineering records
- Department of Archaeology cultural-heritage inventories
