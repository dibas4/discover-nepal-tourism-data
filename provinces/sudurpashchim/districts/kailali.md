## District cautions

- Ghodaghodi must be modeled as a wetland complex with component lakes, not as a single point only.
- Rafting, museums, homestays, boating and riverside facilities require date-stamped operating checks.
- Tharu living-culture records require community consent and should not be reduced to staged attractions.
- Borderland rivers and wildlife corridors need neutral, authoritative mapping.
