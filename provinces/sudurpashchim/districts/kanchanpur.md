## Verification priorities

- Confirm Shuklaphanta gates, safari operations, wetland access, fees and seasonal closures with park authorities.
- Verify Jhilmila and Bedkot lake coordinates, trails, water condition, boating and visitor safety locally.
- Treat Mahakali, Banbasa, Dodhara Chandani and Brahmadev as border or river landscapes with date-stamped access rules.
- Obtain community consent before detailed Rana Tharu and Tharu cultural publication.
- Map Laljhadi and wildlife corridors as geographic areas rather than single-point attractions.
