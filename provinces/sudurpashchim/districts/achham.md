## Verification priorities

- Confirm the official inventory, names, and coordinates of the Ramaroshan lakes and meadows.
- Verify Mangalsen and Panchadewal heritage structures through official heritage records.
- Treat Khaptad as a cross-district protected landscape with separate access-route records.
- Date-stamp flights, roads, trails, festivals, accommodation, and community-tourism operations.
