## District quality notes

- International-border crossings, bridge use, customs procedures, and security rules must be date-stamped.
- Temple names commonly have variant English spellings; preserve aliases until municipality or temple-management confirmation.
- Pancheshwar and Mahakali records are geographic and cross-border entities, not ordinary single-point attractions.
- Small temples, waterfalls, caves, viewpoints, and homestays should not be published as verified until locally checked.
