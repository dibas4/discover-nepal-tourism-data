Province QA rules:
1. Shey Phoksundo National Park is a cross-district protected landscape spanning Dolpa and Mugu; district records represent sectors/access nodes, not duplicate parks.
2. Rara National Park lies mostly in Mugu with a smaller Jumla section; Rara Lake is the principal lake destination within that protected-area hierarchy.
3. Upper Dolpa, Lower Dolpa, Humla restricted zones and Mugum Karmarong use the current Department of Immigration restricted-area framework. Permit fees, agency requirements and covered wards are dynamic operational data.
4. Restricted-area travel is not modeled as ordinary solo trekking. Border crossings, flights, road openings, seasonal passes and remote accommodation are dynamic.
5. Sinja, Kakrebihar and other heritage landscapes are separated from individual monuments/archaeological structures; unsupported legends are not promoted as historical fact.
6. Remote lakes, caves, passes, minor gompas, viewpoints, riverbanks and local shrines remain PARTIAL/HOLD where exact geometry, public access or current visitor readiness lacks authority-grade verification.
7. River valleys, national parks, heritage landscapes and trekking corridors are modeled as area/route objects rather than arbitrary single coordinates.

Primary province anchors verified against Department of National Parks and Wildlife Conservation, Department of Immigration, Nepal Tourism Board/Karnali tourism materials, local governments and heritage authorities where applicable.
