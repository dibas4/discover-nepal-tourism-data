# Jumla deep-research checkpoint

Status: district QA complete.

Canonical Jumla records were reviewed around Sinja/Khas heritage, Chandannath–Khalanga, alpine valleys, lakes, passes, agricultural landscapes and the Jumla side of Rara National Park. Rara National Park is modeled as a cross-district protected area: mostly Mugu, with a smaller Jumla section.

Sinja Valley is treated as a heritage landscape rather than a single point; individual archaeological/inscription/temple assets require structure-level evidence. Apple/agro-tourism, road access, flights, snow conditions and seasonal trekking remain dynamic.

Remote ponds, caves, minor shrines and unnamed viewpoints remain PARTIAL/HOLD where exact location or present visitor readiness lacks strong local/heritage verification.

Primary sources: DNPWC Rara National Park; Nepal Tourism Board Karnali materials; Jumla/local government and heritage authorities.
