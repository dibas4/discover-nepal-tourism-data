# Dolpa deep-research checkpoint

Status: district QA complete.

All canonical Dolpa records were reviewed against the protected-area and restricted-area framework. Shey Phoksundo National Park is modeled as a cross-district protected landscape (Dolpa–Mugu), while Phoksundo Lake, Ringmo, Shey Gompa, Upper Dolpo villages, passes and trekking corridors remain distinct objects where justified.

Current Department of Immigration restricted-area rules are treated as dynamic: Upper Dolpa and Lower Dolpa have different permit regimes and must not be represented by stale hard-coded fees. Restricted-area travel is agency-permit controlled and not modeled as normal unrestricted trekking.

Remote caves, monasteries, passes, seasonal camps and minor local sites remain PARTIAL/HOLD when exact access, coordinates, or present visitor readiness cannot be authority-verified. High-altitude safety and seasonal trail/flight conditions remain dynamic.

Primary sources: Department of National Parks and Wildlife Conservation; Department of Immigration; Nepal Tourism Board and relevant local-government sources.
