## Province QA rules
- ACAP/Annapurna Circuit, Mardi Himal, ABC, Panchase, Ghorepani/Poon Hill, Khopra, Manang and Upper Mustang are modeled as connected route/protected-area entities, not duplicated isolated POIs.
- MCAP/Manaslu/Tsum records are modeled as one protected landscape with villages, monasteries, passes and route nodes beneath it.
- Upper Mustang, Manaslu/Tsum and Nar–Phu restricted-area rules are dynamic and must be read from current Department of Immigration guidance.
- Dhorpatan Hunting Reserve and Chitwan National Park sectors use official protected-area boundaries with district-side access records.
- Cross-district rivers, gorges, passes, bridges and pilgrimage landscapes (Kali Gandaki, Marsyangdi, Seti, Narayani, Devghat, Thorong La, Jaljala, Ramdi, Triveni) use shared canonical entities.
- Commercial operations such as cable cars, bungee/canyon swing, boating and airstrips keep operation status, fees and safety data in current-operation fields.
- Generic community/ethnic/rural clusters remain HOLD until split into named, active, consented visitor products.
- No unverified coordinates are promoted. Seasonal river islands, high-altitude trails, caves and glacier/lake approaches require time-stamped access/safety checks.
