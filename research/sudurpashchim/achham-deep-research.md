## Source anchors
- Khaptad National Park is an official cross-district protected area spanning Achham, Bajhang, Bajura and Doti.
- Ramaroshan is an established Achham destination promoted by Nepal Tourism Board.
- Baidyanath and Sahasreshwar are retained as established pilgrimage identities where supported by national tourism references.

## QA decisions
- Khaptad records are modeled as one shared protected-area entity with district-side access records, not duplicated parks.
- Ramaroshan is modeled as a lake/wetland/cultural landscape rather than a single fake pin.
- Seasonal roads, monsoon access, fair dates, lake conditions and local lodging remain dynamic.
- Minor shrines, caves, undeveloped viewpoints and village clusters without authority-grade location evidence remain PARTIAL/HOLD.
- No unverified coordinates are promoted as publishable.
