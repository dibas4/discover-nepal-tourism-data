## 30. Bhote Koshi–Trishuli headwater corridor
- Type: river corridor / landscape / transport-history context
- District: Rasuwa
- Evidence: Government environmental documentation for the Rasuwagadhi Hydroelectric Project identifies the Bhote Koshi in Gosaikunda Rural Municipality, Rasuwa, as the upper reach of the Trishuli River. Additional basin documentation describes the trans-boundary river entering Nepal from Tibet as Bhote Koshi and continuing as the Trishuli system.
- Modeling: this is a linear river-corridor object, not a rafting-product claim and not a single POI. Keep it distinct from the separate Bhote Koshi river commonly associated with Sindhupalchok.
- Safety: seasonal flood, landslide and hydropower operational conditions are dynamic; never publish static access/safety promises.
- Status: VERIFIED CORRIDOR / ROUTE GEOMETRY & LIVE CONDITIONS PENDING.
