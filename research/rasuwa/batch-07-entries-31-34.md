## 31. Rasuwagadhi Fort

**Disposition:** ESTABLISHED — retain as a distinct historical/fort record.

- Rasuwagadhi is a historic Nepal–Tibet frontier fort in the Timure/Rasuwagadhi border landscape of northern Rasuwa.
- Nepal Tourism Board's Tamang Heritage Trail material identifies Timure as an old Tibet trade-route settlement and explicitly describes the fort at Rasuwagadhi as a historical reminder of centuries of Nepal–Tibet relations.
- A Government-linked study on Bhrikuti identifies Rasuwagadhi in present Gosaikunda Rural Municipality Ward 2, Timure, and notes preservation of historical/archaeological evidence by the Government of Nepal and Nepal Army.
- Keep the fort separate from the modern border/checkpoint record: they share a landscape but represent different visitor objects.

**Planner treatment:** heritage POI; do not imply that proximity to the international border means unrestricted access.

**Sources:**
- Nepal Tourism Board, Rural Tourism / Tamang Heritage Trail: https://trade.ntb.gov.np/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/rural-tourism.pdf
- Study Report on National Luminary Bhrikuti (English translation): https://bma.org.np/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/Study-Report-on-National-Luminary-Bhrikuti.pdf

## 32. Rasuwagadhi border and old trade route

**Disposition:** ESTABLISHED — retain as a historical corridor/border record, not a generic sightseeing POI.

- NTB identifies Timure on the old trade route to Tibet and links the Rasuwagadhi frontier landscape to long-standing Nepal–Tibet relations.
- The contemporary Rasuwagadhi/Timure crossing remains an active international gateway; immigration records and 2026 reporting show cross-border pilgrim movement via Kerung/Gyirong.
- Border operating status, immigration eligibility, bridge/checkpoint access and road conditions are dynamic. The public site must not hard-code unrestricted crossing or current opening status from historical research.
- The 2025 flood/road disruption history reinforces the need for live operational verification before route-planner promises.

**Planner treatment:** historical corridor + controlled international gateway; require a live border/road-status layer for travel planning.

**Sources:**
- Nepal Tourism Board, Rural Tourism / Tamang Heritage Trail: https://trade.ntb.gov.np/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/rural-tourism.pdf
- Nepal Tourism Statistics 2021 (Timure, Rasuwa listed as a land entry point): https://www.tourism.gov.np/files/NOTICE%20MANAGER_FILES/25.pdf
- 2026 immigration/pilgrimage reporting for current-context evidence: Nepal News, 3 May 2026, “Pilgrimage to Kailash Mansarovar begins via Rasuwagadhi border”.
