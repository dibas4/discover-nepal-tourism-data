# Rasuwa Deep Research — Batch 04 (Entries 16–20)

**Canonical progress after this batch:** 20/34 researched/dispositioned.

## 16. Lauribina Pass and ridge
- **Canonical category:** Viewpoints and hill stations
- **Disposition:** VERIFIED / KEEP
- **Identity:** High ridge/pass on the Gosainkunda–Helambu route, commonly rendered Laurebina/Lauribina.
- **Altitude:** Government/NTB material places the pass at about 4,600–4,610 m.
- **Route role:** East of Gosainkunda, linking the lake basin toward Helambu; high-altitude conditions make this a route feature rather than a simple roadside viewpoint.
- **Planner rule:** Model as a high-pass/ridge segment; do not imply year-round easy access.
- **Sources:** Nepal Tourism Board rural-tourism material; Langtang National Park brochure; Nepal government tourism itinerary.

## 17. Cholangpati
- **Canonical category:** Community tourism and notable villages
- **Disposition:** VERIFIED / KEEP AS TRAIL SETTLEMENT
- **Identity:** Lodge/teahouse staging settlement on the Dhunche/Chandanbari–Gosainkunda route below Lauribina.
- **Altitude:** Official park brochure gives about 3,500 m; tourism itinerary material commonly gives roughly 3,600 m.
- **Infrastructure:** Langtang National Park planning documents explicitly list Cholangpati on the prescribed Bharku–Thulosyafru–Chandanbari–Cholangpati–Gosainkunda route and identify temporary campsite capacity there.
- **Planner rule:** Treat as a trail staging/overnight node, not as a large standalone village attraction.
- **Sources:** Langtang National Park Gosainkunda management plan; official tourism itinerary/brochure.

## 18. Sing Gompa / Chandanbari
- **Canonical category:** Religious sites and temples
- **Disposition:** VERIFIED / KEEP
- **Identity:** Monastery/lodge settlement on the classic Dhunche–Gosainkunda approach; official material uses both Sing Gompa/Singh Gomba and Chandanbari.
- **Altitude:** Official tourism material places Chandanbari around 3,330 m; older park brochure places Singh Gomba around 3,000–3,300 m depending on route reference.
- **Route role:** Major overnight/acclimatisation stop before Cholangpati and Lauribina.
- **Planner rule:** Preserve aliases so search resolves Sing Gompa, Singh Gomba and Chandanbari to the same destination record unless later GIS proves distinct visitor nodes.
- **Sources:** Nepal government tourism itinerary; Langtang National Park brochure.

## 19. Dhunche
- **Canonical category:** Major tourist attractions
- **Disposition:** VERIFIED / KEEP AS GATEWAY
- **Identity:** Rasuwa district headquarters and principal road/permit/check-post gateway for Gosainkunda and Langtang National Park routes.
- **Altitude:** Official park/tourism sources place Dhunche roughly around 1,960–2,100 m; avoid forcing one exact elevation onto all map references until GIS normalization.
- **Access role:** NTB describes Gosainkunda access via road from Kathmandu to Dhunche followed by trekking; the park plan lists Dhunche as one of seven Rasuwa check posts issuing park entry permits for the Gosainkunda area.
- **Planner rule:** Model Dhunche as transport + park-entry + trek gateway, not merely a sightseeing POI.
- **Sources:** Nepal Tourism Board Gosainkunda page; Langtang National Park Gosainkunda management plan; park brochure.

## 20. Syabrubesi
- **Canonical category:** Major tourist attractions
- **Disposition:** VERIFIED / KEEP AS GATEWAY
- **Identity:** Major roadhead and trekking gateway for Langtang Valley and the Tamang Heritage Trail; spelling variants include Syafrubesi/Syafrubes.
- **Route role:** NTB identifies the Tamang Heritage Trail as starting from Dhunche or Syabrubesi and the park plan prescribes the Syafrubesi–Godatabela–Langtang–Kyangjin route.
- **Protected-area operations:** The Langtang National Park plan lists Syafrubesi as a Rasuwa check-post location.
- **Planner rule:** Store spelling aliases and treat it as a gateway/transport node. Current road duration, fares, departures and permit enforcement remain live-data fields.
- **Sources:** Nepal Tourism Board Langtang Region page; Langtang National Park Gosainkunda management plan; park brochure.

## Batch QA / modeling notes
- Lauribina is a high-pass route object; do not market it as a low-risk casual viewpoint.
- Cholangpati is best represented as a trail staging settlement.
- Sing Gompa/Chandanbari spelling aliases must be normalized to avoid duplicate records.
- Dhunche and Syabrubesi are both important but serve different route/gateway functions.
- Road condition, bus/jeep schedules, fares, park fees, TIMS/guide requirements, weather and high-pass accessibility are dynamic and must not be frozen from old tourism brochures.
