# Chitwan Deep Research — Batch 01 (Entries 1–10)

Canonical inventory: `provinces/bagmati/districts/chitwan.md`
Coverage: entries 1–10 of 14

## 1. Chitwan National Park
**Disposition:** ESTABLISHED / HIGH PRIORITY.
- UNESCO World Heritage national park and Chitwan's core wildlife destination.
- NTB lists jungle safari, jungle walk, canoe ride and jeep drive, with Kasara as park headquarters.
- Keep fees, opening/access rules and safari operations dynamic because they can change.
Sources: https://ntb.gov.np/chitwan-national-park ; https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/284/

## 2. Bish Hajari birdwatching forests
**Disposition:** ESTABLISHED AS BUFFER-ZONE / WETLAND-FOREST LANDSCAPE.
- Treat as the forest/wildlife landscape associated with the Beeshazar wetland system rather than inventing a separate bounded attraction where authoritative geometry is weak.
- Strong birdwatching/wetland relevance; exact trail/access points require local verification.
Sources: https://ntb.gov.np/en/chitwan ; Ramsar information for Beeshazar and Associated Lakes.

## 3. Sauraha
**Disposition:** ESTABLISHED / HIGH PRIORITY TOURISM GATEWAY.
- NTB explicitly lists Sauraha among places to see in Chitwan.
- Model as the principal eastern tourism/service gateway for park activities and Tharu cultural experiences, not as the national park itself.
- Hotels, activity providers, prices and schedules belong to dynamic data.
Source: https://ntb.gov.np/en/chitwan

## 4. Beeshazar and Associated Lakes
**Disposition:** ESTABLISHED / RAMSAR WETLAND COMPLEX.
- Retain as a wetland-complex record rather than reducing it to one lake pin.
- Important for wetland ecology and birdwatching in the Chitwan buffer-zone landscape.
- Exact component-lake geometry should come from Ramsar/GIS evidence before route-planner precision.
Sources: https://ntb.gov.np/en/chitwan ; Ramsar Sites Information Service.

## 5. Narayani River waterfront
**Disposition:** ESTABLISHED AS RIVER CORRIDOR / WATERFRONT LANDSCAPE.
- Narayani forms after the Kali Gandaki and Trishuli confluence at Devghat and is a major geographic feature of Bharatpur/western Chitwan.
- Do not model the entire river as one POI; individual verified ghats/riverfront facilities can later become child records.
Source: https://ntb.gov.np/en/devghat--chitwan

## 6. Rapti River wildlife corridor
**Disposition:** ESTABLISHED AS RIVER/WILDLIFE CORRIDOR.
- The Rapti forms a key visitor-facing river landscape along the Sauraha/park boundary and is associated with canoe/wildlife viewing activity.
- Keep river condition, boating availability and seasonal safety dynamic.
Source backbone: Nepal Tourism Board Chitwan National Park / Chitwan destination material.

## 7. Jalbire Waterfall
**Disposition:** ESTABLISHED, LOCAL OPERATIONAL DETAILS PENDING.
- Retain as a distinct waterfall/nature stop in Ichchhakamana Rural Municipality.
- Exact current fees, canyoning/operator status, road condition and access facilities require municipality/local verification before publication as operational facts.

## 8. Devghat
**Disposition:** ESTABLISHED / HIGH PRIORITY / CROSS-DISTRICT.
- NTB identifies Devghat as a prominent pilgrimage site at the Kali Gandaki–Trishuli confluence, about 5 km northwest of Narayanghat.
- The confluence becomes the Narayani River.
- Model as one canonical pilgrimage landscape with cross-district relationship metadata rather than duplicate unrelated Chitwan/Tanahun POIs.
- Makar Sankranti festival timing is established culturally, but current event/logistics data remain dynamic.
Source: https://ntb.gov.np/en/devghat--chitwan

## 9. Balmiki Ashram approach
**Disposition:** ESTABLISHED RELIGIOUS DESTINATION; DISTRICT/ACCESS MODEL NEEDS CARE.
- NTB identifies Balmiki Ashram as an added Chitwan attraction and places it in forest at the southwestern corner of Chitwan National Park near the Tribeni river landscape.
- The canonical record is deliberately an 'approach' record because exact administrative attribution, access route and cross-boundary navigation need verification before precise site routing.
- Do not promise unrestricted park/border access.
Sources: https://ntb.gov.np/chitwan-national-park ; https://trade.ntb.gov.np/tourist-destination/chitwan/

## 10. Chepang Hill Trail
**Disposition:** ESTABLISHED / CROSS-DISTRICT ROUTE.
- NTB states the Chitwan Chepang Hill Trail begins at Hugdi in Dhading and ends at Shaktikhor; it can be traveled in reverse from the Chitwan side.
- Cultural experience, birdwatching, mid-hill forests and community accommodation are core characteristics.
- The route must be modeled as a cross-district trail, not assigned wholly to Chitwan.
- Current homestays, trail condition and exact route geometry need live/local verification.
Source: https://ntb.gov.np/en/chepang-hill-trail

## Batch QA decisions
- Entries dispositioned: 10/14.
- Chitwan National Park and Sauraha remain separate: protected area vs tourism gateway.
- Bish Hajari forests and Beeshazar lakes are linked but not duplicate records: surrounding birdwatching forest landscape vs Ramsar wetland complex.
- Narayani and Rapti remain corridor objects rather than fake point POIs.
- Devghat and Chepang Hill Trail carry explicit cross-district metadata.
- Balmiki Ashram routing remains conservative until exact access/administrative geometry is verified.
- Dynamic prices, park rules, operator availability, road/trail condition and schedules are excluded from the static research layer.
