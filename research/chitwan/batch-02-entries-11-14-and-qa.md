## 11. Siraichuli viewpoint
**Disposition:** ESTABLISHED / TREKKING VIEWPOINT.
- Nepal Tourism Board identifies Siraichuli (1,945 m) as one of the highest hills of the Mahabharat range and a major sunrise/sunset viewpoint on the Chepang Hill Trail.
- NTB describes broad Himalayan views including Rolwaling, Gaurishankar, Langtang, Gorkha Himal, Peak 29, Himalchuli, Manaslu, Annapurna and Dhaulagiri, plus views toward Chitwan National Park.
- NTB notes the trek from Hattibang is challenging through rough landscape and vegetation.
- Current trail condition, accommodation, road approach and weather visibility remain dynamic.
Source: https://ntb.gov.np/en/chepang-hill-trail

## 12. Upper Dang Gadhi / Uppardang Gadi
**Disposition:** ESTABLISHED / HISTORICAL FORT + VIEWPOINT.
- NTB states Uppardang Gadi was Chitwan's headquarters until early 1962 and that remains of the historic fort survive.
- NTB gives an elevation of about 1,275 m and associates the fort with Shatru Bhanjan Shah, eldest son of Bahadur Shah.
- Normalize spelling variants Upper Dang Gadhi / Uppardang Gadi / Upardang Gadhi under one canonical record.
- Do not use tourism-marketing claims such as 'highest peak' when the authoritative NTB description treats it primarily as a historic fort/viewpoint.
Source: https://ntb.gov.np/en/chepang-hill-trail

## 13. Meghauli wildlife tourism area
**Disposition:** ESTABLISHED / HIGH PRIORITY WESTERN PARK GATEWAY.
- Nepal Tourism Board identifies Meghauli as the western entrance of Chitwan National Park on the Rapti and Narayani rivers and within the buffer-zone region.
- NTB highlights Tharu culture, wildlife viewing, birdwatching, jungle activities and sunset at Golghat, the Rapti–Narayani confluence.
- Model Meghauli as a tourism/community gateway rather than as a duplicate of Chitwan National Park.
- Hotels, homestays, activity providers, prices and operating status are dynamic.
Source: https://ntb.gov.np/en/meghauli
