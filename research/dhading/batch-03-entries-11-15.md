## 12. Ganga Jamuna Waterfall

**Disposition:** ESTABLISHED — major natural and religious tourism destination.

- Ganga Jamuna Rural Municipality's own transport master plan states that the municipality is named after Gangajamuna Jharana, describing it as a popular Dhading waterfall and a religiously/culturally important destination visited by thousands during the Ekadashi mela in Mangsir.
- Public Service Broadcasting/Radio Nepal independently describes Ganga Jamuna waterfall as a well-known religious and tourism destination in Dhading and documents the annual Haribodhani Ekadashi gathering.
- Preserve the waterfall as the natural-attraction object; do not collapse it into the pilgrimage/festival record below.

**Planner treatment:** major natural POI with pilgrimage relationship metadata. Seasonal trail/road condition and exact safe viewing access require live/local checks.

**Sources:**
- Ganga Jamuna Rural Municipality, Rural Municipality Transport Master Plan: https://gangajamunamun.gov.np/sites/gangajamunamun.gov.np/files/RMTMP-Gangajamuna-RM_0.pdf
- Radio Nepal / Public Service Broadcasting, 23 Nov 2023: https://radionepalonline.com/en/2023/11/23/367664.html

## 13. Ganga Jamuna pilgrimage area

**Disposition:** ESTABLISHED — retain separately from the waterfall.

- The pilgrimage landscape is centered on worship associated with Ganga Jamuna Mai and the waterfall site.
- Radio Nepal documents the annual festival on Haribodhani Ekadashi, when devotees gather at the base of the waterfall, bathe and worship.
- The rural municipality's official planning document confirms the Ekadashi mela and the destination's religious/cultural importance.
- Festival dates vary by the lunar calendar and must not be hard-coded as a fixed Gregorian date.

**Planner treatment:** religious/pilgrimage POI linked to the waterfall; add festival-event data through a dynamic calendar layer.

**Sources:** same official/local sources as entry 12.

## 14. Tripurasundari Mai Temple

**Disposition:** ESTABLISHED, but exact temple-level operational details need local-source strengthening.

- The canonical inventory identifies Tripurasundari Mai as a regionally important goddess shrine associated with Tripurasundari Rural Municipality.
- Secondary district tourism references consistently identify Tripurasundari Mai Temple in the northern Salyankot/Tripurasundari area of Dhading.
- Avoid confusing this Dhading shrine with other Nepal temples named Tripura/Tripurasundari, especially the better-indexed Baitadi and Dolakha sites.
- Exact ward, entrance pin, ritual calendar, priest/contact information and current road access should be verified from the municipality/temple authority before a detailed public guide is published.

**Planner treatment:** established religious POI, with a disambiguation key `Tripurasundari Mai Temple — Dhading`.

## 15. Salyantar Plateau

**Disposition:** ESTABLISHED LANDSCAPE / VIEWPOINT REGION.

- Salyantar is retained as a broad elevated plateau/settlement landscape in Tripurasundari Rural Municipality rather than a single artificial viewpoint pin.
- The tourism value is the plateau landscape and views toward the Budhi Gandaki valley and surrounding hill/Himalayan terrain.
- Exact viewpoint coordinates should be modeled as child records only when individually verified; the plateau itself should remain an area/landscape object.
- Road condition, public transport and best viewpoint access are dynamic and require current local verification.

**Planner treatment:** landscape/area destination, not a point-only attraction.
