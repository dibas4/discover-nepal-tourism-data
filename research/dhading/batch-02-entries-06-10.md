## 06. Sertung Village
**Disposition:** ESTABLISHED — retain as a distinct community/cultural destination.
- Sertung is part of the core northern Dhading/Ruby Valley cultural landscape.
- Government Film Development Board material says Ruby Valley Rural Municipality was formed by combining Tipling, Sertung and Lapa villages and describes the wider area as home to Tamang, Gurung, Ghale and Dalit communities.
- Tourism itineraries use Sertung as a distinct village stop rather than merely a generic point on the trail.
**Planner treatment:** community destination; verify current homestay/teahouse inventory before promising accommodation.

## 07. Chalish Village
**Disposition:** ESTABLISHED — retain as a distinct community-tourism and trekking stop.
- Government tourism/filming material explicitly places Chalise/Chalish Village on the Ruby Valley Circuit after Tipling and before the Hindung/Ganesh Himal Base Camp side of the route.
- It also appears on the return/exit network toward Borang and lower Dhading.
- Preserve spelling aliases such as Chalish/Chalise/Chalish Gaon rather than creating duplicate POIs.
**Planner treatment:** village + trekking node; accommodation and local-program status remain dynamic.

## 08. Borang Village
**Disposition:** ESTABLISHED AS ROUTE/COMMUNITY NODE; FACILITIES NEED CURRENT VERIFICATION.
- Government Film Development Board material explicitly includes Borang on the Ruby Valley circuit/return route and connects the route onward toward Jharlang and Dhadingbesi.
- Current commercial trekking itineraries also use Borang as a road/trek transition and village stop, supporting its continuing practical relevance.
- Do not overstate homestay availability from itinerary marketing alone.
**Planner treatment:** village + route gateway; current lodging/road status should come from a live/local layer.

## 09. Lapa Village
**Disposition:** ESTABLISHED CULTURAL/GEOGRAPHIC RECORD; TOURISM SERVICES NEED VERIFICATION.
- Government Film Development Board material identifies Lapa as one of the villages combined in forming Ruby Valley Rural Municipality.
- Ganesh Himal Tourism Development Committee identifies Lapa among the northern Dhading communities involved in regional tourism development.
- Trekking products also route through Lapa, but current accommodation capacity and exact preferred access route should not be hard-coded from commercial itineraries.
**Planner treatment:** community destination with local-service verification flag.

## 10. Jharlang Village
**Disposition:** ESTABLISHED — retain as a separate village and southern/northern route connector.
- Government Film Development Board material explicitly says the Ruby Valley return route continues via Borang to Jharllang/Jharlang, from where Dhadingbesi can be reached by vehicle.
- Ganesh Himal Tourism Development Committee also identifies Jharlang among the northern Dhading communities historically organized around tourism development.
- Keep Jharlang distinct from Darkha; the canonical inventory correctly separates them.
**Planner treatment:** community/route gateway; verify exact current road condition and visitor lodging locally.
