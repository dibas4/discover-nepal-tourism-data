## Strongly grounded backbone

Government and official tourism evidence strongly supports the district's tourism backbone, including Ruby Valley/Ganesh Himal trekking, Pangsang and northern settlements, Ganga Jamuna, Siddhalek, major river landscapes and other established destinations. The Dhading District Administration Office itself identifies Ganga Jamuna waterfall, Jharlang hot spring, caves, Siddhalek and the district's river/natural landscapes among Dhading's defining features.

## Content QA

- guaranteed accommodation;
- unrestricted trail/road access;
- exact journey duration under all conditions;
- current ritual/festival dates without fresh verification;
- precise heritage remains where records are flagged HOLD;
- current rafting or adventure-sport availability without operator/safety confirmation.
