## 31. Dhola Mandali Temple

**Disposition:** ESTABLISHED — retain as a distinct religious/pilgrimage destination.

- Jwalamukhi Rural Municipality explicitly lists Dhola Mandali among the municipality's religious, archaeological and tourism-potential places, alongside Jwalamukhi Mai, Maidi Kot, Khari Kot and Chainpur Kot.
- The municipality was formed partly from the former Dhola VDC, giving strong administrative grounding to the Dhola identity.
- Local reporting places Dhola Basaha Mandali Mai in Jwalamukhi Rural Municipality Ward 4 and describes it as a religious/historical temple near Dhadingbesi. This is useful supporting evidence, but mutable ritual schedules and exact visitor operations should not be hard-coded from news reporting.
- The canonical record's note about distinctive ritual traditions is plausible, but detailed ritual claims should remain source-qualified and should not be generalized without local verification.

**Planner treatment:** religious POI. Static layer can publish identity, municipality/ward context and pilgrimage significance. Dynamic/local layer should verify road access, festival dates, ritual schedule, parking, visitor facilities and any restrictions before trip-planner promises.
