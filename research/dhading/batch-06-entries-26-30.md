## 26. Dhadingbesi

**Disposition:** ESTABLISHED — retain as district-headquarters/gateway record.

- Dhadingbesi is the practical headquarters and service gateway for central and northern Dhading and lies within Nilkantha Municipality.
- Nilkantha Municipality's official introduction confirms the municipality was formed from Neelakantha, Sankosh, Muralibhanjyang and Sunaulabazar areas; Dhadingbesi functions as the district/municipal administrative center.
- Model it as a transport/service base rather than pretending the town itself is one bounded tourist attraction.

**Planner treatment:** gateway/service node. Accommodation, buses, road status, offices and operating hours are dynamic fields.

**Sources:**
- Nilkantha Municipality: https://neelakanthamun.gov.np/en/node

## 27. Bhairabi Temple, Sunaula Bazar

**Disposition:** ESTABLISHED, but exact public pin/visitor details need final GIS/local confirmation.

- The canonical inventory identifies Bhairabi Temple as a prominent hill shrine in the Sunaula Bazar area.
- Nilkantha Municipality officially confirms Sunaulabazar as one of the areas incorporated into the municipality.
- A documented Dhadingbesi–Sunaula Bazar–Bhairabi Mandir road project provides additional infrastructure-level evidence for the named temple destination.

**Sources:**

## 29. Siddha Than / Siddha Baba Temple

**Disposition:** ESTABLISHED — strong official/local-government and provincial tourism evidence.

- Siddhalek Rural Municipality's official introduction states that the municipality is named after the Siddha Baba temple, which has long been a center of faith for local residents.
- Bagmati Province tourism research describes Siddha Baba Temple in Siddhalek Rural Municipality as a hilltop Hindu shrine at approximately 1,350 m, with panoramic views toward Ganesh, Manaslu and Langtang ranges and hiking access.
- Normalize `Siddha Than`, `Siddhasthan`, and `Siddha Baba Temple` as aliases unless field/GIS work proves distinct nearby objects.

**Planner treatment:** religious POI + hiking destination. Exact trail geometry and entrance pin should come from GIS/field verification.

## 30. Siddhalek ridge

**Disposition:** ESTABLISHED AS A LANDSCAPE/VIEWPOINT RIDGE, not a single arbitrary pin.

- Bagmati Province tourism research treats the Siddhalek area as a hiking and viewpoint destination associated with Siddha Baba Temple and panoramic Himalayan views.
- The same provincial study notes tourism-promotion initiatives such as `Hike to Siddhalek` and the Lower Ganesh Himal and Manaslu circuit concept.
- Academic geotourism research also identifies the scenic Siddhalek Danda ridge as part of the area's tourism potential.
- Keep the ridge record separate from Siddha Baba Temple: the temple is the religious POI, while the ridge is the broader landscape/hiking/viewpoint product.

**Planner treatment:** ridge/landscape route object; do not assign a fake single-point coordinate. Trailheads and route geometry require GIS/field validation.

**Sources:**
- Bagmati Province tourism study, Volume 4: https://tourism.bagamati.gov.np/media/pdf/compressed_book_volume_4.pdf
- Siddhalek Rural Municipality: https://siddhalekmun.gov.np/
