## 16. Narsingha Dham

**Disposition:** ESTABLISHED WITH LOCAL-DETAIL FLAG.

- Retain Narsingha Dham as a distinct religious destination associated with Salyantar, Tripurasundari Rural Municipality.
- Treat it as a religious complex/shrine landscape rather than conflating it with the broader Salyantar Plateau record.
- Exact entrance coordinates, current temple-management contacts, festival calendar, accommodation and road access should be verified from local-government/temple sources before detailed CMS publication.

**Planner treatment:** religious POI; use conservative location data until local verification is complete.

## 17. Budhi Gandaki River valley

**Disposition:** ESTABLISHED — retain as a river-valley landscape/corridor.

- The Budhi Gandaki forms the important western Dhading/Gorkha boundary landscape and is not a single-point attraction.
- It shapes western Dhading access and the Salyantar/Tripurasundari landscape.
- River condition, bridge status and road access are dynamic and should not be frozen into static destination copy.

**Planner treatment:** natural corridor/landscape; route geometry and crossing points belong in GIS/operations data.

## 18. Ankhu Khola valley

**Disposition:** ESTABLISHED — retain as a river-valley corridor.

- Ankhu Khola is a major river system in Dhading; hydropower documentation places the project area in Dhading around the former Salyankot/Marpak area and gives a catchment of roughly 633 km².
- Treat the tourism record as the broader valley/corridor, not as a hydropower attraction.
- Do not publish hydropower infrastructure as visitor-accessible unless separately authorized and verified.

**Planner treatment:** natural landscape/corridor; exact recreational access points require local verification.

**Sources:**
- Ankhu Khola Hydropower project introduction and salient features.
- Government of Nepal Department of Electricity Development project records for Upper Ankhu Khola.

## 19. Trishuli River rafting corridor

**Disposition:** ESTABLISHED — major adventure-tourism corridor.

- Nepal Tourism Board identifies the Trishuli as Nepal's most popular raftable river and grades it approximately 3+ in its general rafting guidance.
- NTB gives the usual rafting seasons as October to mid-December and March to early May; actual conditions vary with discharge and weather.
- NTB also identifies Charaundi Canyon along the Trishuli in Dhading, strengthening the district's established adventure-tourism role.
- Keep this record separate from Benighat and Malekhu, which are land-based gateway/stop records.

**Planner treatment:** adventure corridor; live operator, river-flow, weather and safety checks are mandatory before presenting a trip as available.

**Sources:**
- Nepal Tourism Board — Rafting / Kayaking / Canoeing.
- Nepal Tourism Board — Canyoning (Charaundi Canyon, Dhading).

## 20. Benighat river gateway

**Disposition:** ESTABLISHED AS GATEWAY, NOT A SINGLE ATTRACTION.

- Retain Benighat as a practical highway/river gateway associated with the Trishuli corridor and southern Dhading movement.
- Its value for the route planner is access, staging, food/lodging and connections rather than a fabricated monument-style POI.
- Current rafting put-in/take-out points, operator bases, highway delays and local facilities must be refreshed operationally.

**Planner treatment:** gateway/service node linked to Trishuli adventure products.
