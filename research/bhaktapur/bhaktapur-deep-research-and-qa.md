## 1. Bhaktapur Durbar Square
**Disposition:** ESTABLISHED / MAJOR / UNESCO HERITAGE CORE.
- Nepal Tourism Board identifies Bhaktapur Durbar Square as a UNESCO World Heritage Site and describes the palace/temple ensemble including the 55-Window Palace, Golden Gate, Lion Gate, Bhupatindra Malla statue and Vatsala Temple.
- Bhaktapur Municipality independently describes the square as the city's principal heritage ensemble and names the same core monuments.
- Model as a heritage-zone record; museum/facility components should remain child POIs, not duplicates.
- Current entry fee is time-sensitive and should come from the live NTB heritage-fee table at publication time.
Sources:
- https://ntb.gov.np/en/bhaktapur
- https://www.bhaktapurmun.gov.np/en/node/4
- https://ntb.gov.np/plan-your-trip/before-you-come/heritage-site-entry-fees

## 2. Dattatreya Square

- Bhaktapur Municipality describes Dattatraya Square as the oldest part of the city and an open museum for wood carving; it also identifies the Woodcarving Museum and Brass & Bronze Museum in the area.

## 3. Thimi heritage town

- Bhaktapur Municipality lists Thimi among ideal places to visit around Bhaktapur.
- Madhyapur Thimi Municipality's own cultural messaging and programs reinforce the town's living cultural identity.
- Model as a town/heritage-area record, not a single-point attraction.
