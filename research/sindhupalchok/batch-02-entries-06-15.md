## 08. Panch Pokhari
**Disposition:** ESTABLISHED — major sacred high-altitude lake destination.
- Government district material identifies Panch Pokhari among Sindhupalchok's famous lakes.
- Government Film Development Board documentation places Panch Pokhari in Thangpal Rural Municipality and describes the five-water-body landscape, nearby Bhotang/Manekhark/Melamchi, trekking access and basic trail facilities.
- Treat altitude as approximate because official/official-adjacent sources vary around 4,100–4,150 m.

Sources:
- https://daosindhulpalchok.moha.gov.np/en/post/district-introduction-1
- https://film.gov.np/destination/51

## 13. Langtang National Park sector
**Disposition:** ESTABLISHED — cross-district protected-area sector.
- Langtang National Park officially states that the park covers parts of Rasuwa, Nuwakot and Sindhupalchok; 36% of the park area lies in Sindhupalchok.
- Do not present the park as belonging exclusively to Sindhupalchok.
- Park fees, entry procedures, route restrictions and operating rules belong in a live protected-area layer.

Source: https://langtangnationalpark.gov.np/en/
