The district has a well-supported tourism backbone across several categories:

- Helambu trekking region and major Yolmo settlements.
- Ama Yangri and Helambu pilgrimage/viewpoint landscape.
- Panch Pokhari sacred lakes and trekking route.
- Jugal Himal mountain region.
- Langtang National Park sector.
- Bhairav Kunda and Tatopani.
- Kodari–Tatopani historical border corridor.
- Bhote Koshi rafting, canyoning and bungee/canyon-swing products.
- Sukute/Sunkoshi river recreation corridor.
- Gaurati Bhimsen, Tauthali Tripura Sundari and other religious/cultural records.
- Melamchi, Indrawati and Bahrabise gateway/river landscapes.
